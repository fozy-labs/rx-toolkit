# Carriers — what a block, an ordering, a renewal event and an advance become here

> Loaded when: The carrier is known and before the block ordering is fixed — mandatory when the carrier is not a plain screen (banner, identity, print, HUD).

Slot 4 asks four questions: what counts as a block here; what the block ordering is; how many renewal events exist and where; is advance live. On a scrolling application screen the answers are so nearly automatic that they get taken rather than derived. On any other carrier they are not, and taking them is how the whole apparatus arrives somewhere it does not apply.

This file does not hold a carrier catalogue. **Carrier class is not an input to any derivation here.** What decides is a small set of properties that a carrier either has or does not, and those properties are what you read off the brief.
RU: решает не название носителя, а его свойства; класс носителя сам по себе ничего не выводит.

## The five properties that actually decide

Answer these five before you answer slot 4's four. Each is a fact about the brief, not a judgement.

1. **Does the carrier renew?** List the hard cognitive acts available on it — a click, a scroll, reading a paragraph, filling a field. Hovering is not one, looking is not one, and a cliché icon is not one. The count may be zero.
2. **How many passes over the contact window?** One, a handful, or daily-and-indefinite. This is the multiplier on every per-pass cost in the model, and it is the single number that most often inverts a verdict.
3. **Is the carrier entered, or worked through?** A carrier the user *arrives at* and leaves behaves differently from a surface they operate. This is what decides whether an atemporal device is a one-time asset or a per-pass toll.
4. **Is there a next step the carrier can lead to?** If yes, the ordering has a terminus and advance has somewhere to be spent. If no, the ordering ends inside the frame and the advance question changes shape.
5. **Is the viewing distance and physical size fixed by the world rather than by the layout?** Where it is, relate dimensions to a person standing at their actual distance rather than to the screen.

## Deriving slot 4 from the five

**What counts as a block.** The rule does not change with the carrier: anything visually separated is a block and takes a step in the ordering, and outlines and rules are blocks in their own right. What changes is what "visually separated" reaches. On a carrier grasped in one look, the unit of separation is the spot, not the component — defocus to colour spots and count what survives. On a mark, the blocks are the entities the eye records: silhouettes, internal shapes, gaps, displaced groups.

**What the ordering is.** The block ordering is arbitrary to take and simultaneously the actual chain of actualisation, parameterised by hierarchy. That definition survives every carrier. What varies is whether the ordering is *within* the frame or *across* frames — which is slot 3's question, and if slot 3 said the solution is in the linking of orderings, then the carrier you are holding is one node of a larger ordering and its internal ordering is the smaller problem.

**How many renewal events, and where.** Read them straight off property 1. Then read the consequence: **with zero renewal events, the frame's accent budget is never reset, so there is no loop to diagnose, and dimming versus removing is no longer the choice it is elsewhere.** The loop diagnosis, the renewal law, and every argument that turns on cost accruing per pass are inapplicable, and saying so is the correct output.
RU: нет обновляющих событий — нет петли; закон обновления и все аргументы «цена копится на каждом проходе» здесь неприменимы.

**Is advance live.** Advance is live wherever there is a next step the user may decline to take. It is **switched off** under absolute temporality — the user has already decided and there will be no drop — and you write OFF and say why rather than scoring it low. On a carrier with no next step at all (property 4 negative), advance is not off but exhausted at the frame: the whole carrier is one joint, and the walk that normally runs joint by joint collapses to a single question — did this frame add more than it spent, once.

## Worked instances, each with its counter

These are instances of the derivation above, not entries in a table to look up. Each one is here because it inverts something.

**A carrier with one pass and no renewal events.** Every per-pass argument goes silent: the legend-against-duplicate comparison inverts, because a loop charged once is not a loop; an atemporal device is charged once and is therefore affordable at a cost that would be ruinous on a working surface; a competitor that would form a loop elsewhere simply competes once and loses or wins. What survives at full strength: exclusivity, span, self-similarity, sign-load, and advance as one joint.
The confirmed ordering for this case where the carrier must intercept a gaze — **attract → inform → lead away** — is an *order*, not a proportion. Proportions quoted for it drift between tellings and are illustrations, not norms.
RU: триада «привлечь → проинформировать → увести» — порядок, не пропорция.
*Counter.* One pass is a property of this contact, not of the medium. The same carrier met daily by staff has renewal events and a pass count, and the whole inversion above runs backwards.

**A mark, and the identity around it.** Slot 4's block question resolves to the entities the eye records, and the ordering is internal. The decisive move is upstream of all of it: ask where the sign is carried in the wider system. If it is carried outside the mark, a low-sign-load mark costs nothing; if the mark is the only carrier, low sign-load is a bet and the Ledger must say it is a bet. Three ways to build a mark — high sign-load, low, balanced — are equally valid, and the choice derives from that question, not from ambition.
The remove-the-mark probe is available and its verdict is **not** settled: the author runs it in both directions and does not reconcile them. Run it, report which reading your result supports, and treat the verdict as a hypothesis with its falsifier named. See `references/sign.md`.
*Counter.* A mark is not automatically a one-pass carrier. A mark that sits in a product's own working surface is met at that surface's contact frequency and inherits its per-pass economics.

**A physically sized carrier — a kiosk, a wall panel, a small handheld.** Property 5 is live: relate dimensions to a person at their actual distance rather than to the screen, and ask for the diagonal before deciding anything about size. When porting a pattern from one device to another, ask which resource is in surplus on the *target* — width on a desktop, height on a handheld — and let the surplus, not the source layout, set the arrangement.
Boundary, stated by the author about his own claim: the effect where something too large at close range falls out of the field of view he calls uncategorised. Use it as a hypothesis and never derive from it.
*Counter.* Carrier-and-diagonal reasoning is confirmed for **physical screens only**. Do not extend it to a printed sheet or an object by analogy; that extension is not in the corpus.

**A surface under stress, with information whose omission decides an outcome.** Depth runs inverse to criticality, so a datum that decides the result belongs near the look-only contour however rarely it is consulted. Second, independent constraint: module positions are learned in a calm state and used under stress, so the path that taught the layout may not be the path that uses it.
*Counter.* Criticality is a property of the datum in this scenario, not of the domain. The same reasoning run in the other direction moves reference knowledge that decides nothing in the moment out to on-demand.
Forbidden here specifically: the 3–5-second checking cadence, which is single-occurrence material from one combat-game interface and must not be generalised to any argument about layout. See `references/limits.md`.

**Print, and anything else consumed as a graphic in a frame but not on a screen.** The model's demarcation is by carrier: it reads what is drawn as a layout or a graphic and consumed by the eye inside a frame. Print is inside that demarcation, so the frame-level apparatus reaches it — spot-level blocks, span, exclusivity, self-similarity, sign-load, sufficiency, and advance as one joint. What does not reach it: everything indexed to renewal events, and anything about physical screens.
**Honest gap.** The corpus's remarks about interfaces being printed, about the left third of the colour box, and about a cheat colour belong to a **guest**, not the author, and must not be attributed or used as rules. Beyond the demarcation statement, the model grounds no print-specific verdict. Say that rather than reconstructing one.

## The INAPPLICABLE line, computed

Once slot 4 is filled, the INAPPLICABLE line in the output contract is a computation, not a composition. Walk these four preconditions and print every law whose precondition this carrier fails:

| Precondition, read off slot 4 | What is void when it fails |
|---|---|
| Renewal events exist | the renewal law; the loop diagnosis; every argument in which a cost accrues per pass; the choice between dimming and removing |
| A next step exists that the user may decline | advance, and the four decisions advance alone settles |
| Absolute temporality does **not** hold | advance again — under absolute temporality it is switched off, and you say so |
| An audience is named in slot 7 | every sign verdict; sign-load without a named audience is undefined, not low |
| The carrier falls inside the demarcation — drawn as a layout or graphic, consumed by the eye in a frame | all of it |

Print the failures, with the property that produced each. If none fail, print that none fail and name the property you checked. A blank or generic INAPPLICABLE line is a template, and templates fail the output contract.

## The exit this file is also for

Objects, speech, process, hiring, organisational design are outside the demarcation. **"The model does not reach here" is a legitimate output.** Dressing a preference in this vocabulary is not, and the author marks his own excursions past the line as hunch or joke; mark yours the same way or stay silent.
RU: «модель сюда не достаёт» — законный ответ; вкус, одетый в этот словарь, — нет.

If you have taken the exit, the derivation still owes something: name which of the five properties made the carrier unreadable, and what would have to be true for the model to reach it. That is a finding, and it is more useful to the user than an answer manufactured out of the vocabulary.
