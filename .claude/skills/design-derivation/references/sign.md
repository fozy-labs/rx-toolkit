# Sign-load and the Atemporal Reading

> Loaded when: Filling the sign column — always, in the same pass as `references/charges.md`; and for any brief where impression is the deliverable, or the complaint is "it looks bland" or "it looks generic".

## What sign-load is

Two components, read as one value, never separately: how many interpretants this named audience takes off the form unambiguously, and what affective response those interpretants give.
RU: знаковость — число однозначно считываемых интерпретант плюс сила аффектного отклика у этой аудитории.

Fill each sign cell with both: a count (including "one, and it is the wrong one", or "none"), and a named response. A cell holding only an adjective is not filled — send it back.

Do not build a media ladder under this. The corpus carries no scale running from flat vector up to a rendered scene; it carries one evaluative pair — a deficient mark against a plot object — and a pair is not an axis.
RU: шкалы «плоский вектор → цвет → эффекты → 3D» нет; есть оценочная пара «дефицитарный знак ↔ сюжетка».

## The audience is inside the definition

Sign-load is not a property of the form. The same form gives one audience three unambiguous interpretants and another audience none, and the count is the thing you are measuring. Name the audience in the Ledger before you count; an unnamed audience makes the count unfalsifiable.
RU: аудитория входит в определение знаковости, а не стоит рядом с ней.

Count the clichés this audience already carries, not the ones you carry. A device saturated in the environment your audience actually looks at delivers its interpretant and a second, unwanted one about the sender. Whether that argues for taking the mass device or against it is not settled here — it depends on whether the element must be found or must intercept: see `references/operations.md`.

## Orthogonality

Sign-load is orthogonal to charge. A high-sign object can sit microscopic in a corner and stay high-sign; a maximal-charge block can carry zero interpretants. Never repair one by moving the other.
RU: знаковость ортогональна акценту: высокознаковое может стоять микроскопическим в углу.

Sign-load is orthogonal to detail. Detail is a count of densities; sign-load is a count of read meanings. Their product is what you are usually reaching for.
RU: знаковость + детализация = сюжетность.

Because they are orthogonal, adding detail to a low-sign form raises cost without raising load. That is parasitic density under a different name — see `references/surface.md`.

## Assigning slots: the sufficiency principle

Sign-load is distributed across slots, not maximised. High sign-load goes into the plot object; low sign-load goes deliberately onto the background and onto anything that must not compete.
RU: принцип достаточности — высокознаковое в сюжетный объект, малознаковое сознательно на фон.

The principle has two bounds and you must find both for this object:
- Upper bound — tighten the crop mentally until the object stops being recognised. Past that point detail is being paid for out of another block's charge.
- Lower bound — cut detail down to the threshold where the sign still reads. A flock reads at about five birds (illustration, not a norm); swipe points run down to no more than three (confirmed limit; the six-point figure is forbidden — see `references/limits.md`).
- Then take the invariant part — the portion that transmits nothing because it never changes — off frame entirely.
RU: неизменную, ничего не транслирующую часть уводить за кадр.

## Where sign comes from

Extract it from the product or from the business's own mythology and press it into the interface, rather than sourcing it from the market. A mechanism, a material, a gesture the product actually contains, primitivised, then propagated across the layout as one system instead of one picture.
RU: знаковая экспансия — знак, вынутый из предметной области и распространённый на всю графику.
Test the extracted sign for reuse: does it bend, and does it lead the eye along the whole block ordering, or does it work in exactly one place.

When the client supplies expensive raster content, scan it for signs instead of framing it as finished. Pull one out, propagate it, and note that text usually shortens without loss of informativeness ratio once the sign carries part of the load.
RU: дорогой растр заказчика не фреймить, а сканировать на знаки.

When the subject is abstract and there is no plot object to find, construct plot-load out of the object's own form — a mechanic that grows from the shape already present rather than a scene laid over it, and assign it a sign immediately.
RU: сюжетность конструируется из формы самого объекта, а не накладывается поверх.

## Raising load without raising detail

Run the form through the circle / square / triangle filter: if every element is one type, mix types and introduce the contrasting figure at a small number of points (illustration — the count is derived from how many exclusive layers slot 7 will carry, not fixed at one or two).
RU: фильтр «круг — квадрат — треугольник» — смешивать типы точечно.

Self-similarity is what caps load: a repeated rule is predicted after its second occurrence, and predicted structures fuse into one layer and exhaust faster. Repeating an element multiplies visible complexity at zero gain in load.
RU: нагрузку создаёт различие, а не количество.

Correction, stated here because the extraction inverted it: symmetry is itself a self-similar structure. The load does not come from having symmetry — it comes from **breaking** it, by displacing a group and thereby generating a new internal distance and a new internal form without adding a single element.
RU: симметрия сама относится к самоподобным структурам; нагрузку даёт её нарушение, а не наличие.

A device introduced once reads as an accent on that one element, not as a language. It needs an answer — a second occurrence on a form of similar geometry. Then check the third occurrence for excess: the ceiling is set by the complexity of the sign, and a simple mark tolerates fewer variations of its own rule.
RU: приёму нужен «ответ» — второе вхождение; третье проверяется на избыточность.

Build with the contrast ladder — duplicate, then break symmetry by position, then contrast by scale, by shape, by brightness — starting from a self-similar field and adding exclusive layers one at a time, aligning each to what is already there. Emptiness between blocks counts as a form and takes a rung on the same ladder.
RU: пустота между блоками — тоже форма.

The rungs are ordered by **ascending accent price**: each one further up buys more difference and costs more charge. Which rung you stop at is therefore not a matter of how far up you can get — it is derived from the charge budget slot 8 leaves after the required span is met. A rung taken above that budget is paid for out of accent #1, so name that transfer or do not take the rung. Two briefs with the same form and different required spans stop at different rungs, and each can say why.

## Reducing a variant to one number

Decompose the form into the entities the eye will actually record — silhouettes, internal shapes, gaps, displaced groups. Give each a percentage of self-similarity against what has already been seen. Sum to one number: the volume of attention this variant loads. Compare variants by that number rather than by preference; it is the only comparison here that reproduces.
RU: разложить форму на сущности, каждой присвоить процент самоподобия и свести вариант к одному числу загрузки.

Do not carry the number across briefs. It is an instance of a derivation, not a score.

## Colour as a code

Before assigning colour a meaning, check two things: whether that colour is already occupied by another predicate in this system, and whether the chosen hue drags a parasitic connotation the audience will read whether you meant it or not.
RU: предикат должен быть свободен и не тянуть паразитную коннотацию.
A code that fails either check is not a weak code — it is a second, competing interpretant, and it belongs in the sign cell as such.

## Delegating sign beyond the row of blocks

When you need more sign-load than the hierarchy of the frame permits, stop competing for the charge budget and find a carrier outside the row of blocks: framing, plot-bearing borders, the space between frames.
RU: искать носитель знаковости вне ряда блоков — обрамление, межфреймовое пространство.
This is the standard resolution when the sign column and the charge column contradict each other. Do not resolve that contradiction by lowering the accent.

## Low sign-load is a strategy, not a defect

There are three equally valid ways to build a mark — high sign-load, low sign-load, and balanced — and the choice is derived from where the sign is carried in the wider system, not from ambition.
RU: малознаковость — легитимная стратегия, а не порок.

Low sign-load becomes a defect in exactly one condition: when it occupies the plot slot in order to fill an empty area. That is deficiency dressed as restraint.
RU: дефицитарность — обеднённое решение, выдаваемое за минимализм.

Two repairs, and they are not interchangeable — pick by which column is short: take the mark into high load by **number** (more entities, more difference between them), or by **added detail** (more read meaning per entity). Number is the cheaper repair when the charge budget is tight, because it does not raise density on any single block.

If a mark needs its own text beside it in order to mean anything, its sign-load has been transferred to its environment. That is a legitimate bet — it is also a bet, and the Ledger must say so.

## Abstraction is not sign-load

A structure that is pleasant and reads nothing is abstraction, not a sign. Perception reaches for sign-load and does not have the time to construct one for you.
RU: восприятие стремится обрести знаковость, но времени на это нет.
Do not test this by deleting the text and asking whether the plot survives — that experiment exists in the corpus with the opposite conclusion (the logos is primary, and what remains without it is a shell of surplus densities). See `references/limits.md`.

## The empty carrier: a fork, not a rule

An empty banner, screen, or mark is not automatically filled and not automatically left. There are two legal cures and the author uses both in one sitting:
- spread the hierarchy — widen the span between extremes and let emptiness become the form (see `references/charges.md`);
- add sign and plot-load — put a plot object into the empty slot.
RU: пустота лечится либо разносом иерархии, либо добавлением знака и сюжетки.
Choose from the Ledger: if the required span is unmet, the first; if the span is met and the affect carrier is "nobody", the second. Emitting "it needs something" without picking a branch is not an answer.

## T5 — the atemporal reading: carrier and stretch decide, never product type

An imperative product is not thereby barred from an atemporal layer, and a voluntary one is not thereby entitled to it. What decides is **who carries the affect** and **which stretch of the scenario the gesture sits on**.
RU: решает не тип продукта, а носитель атемпорали и участок сценария.

Apply in this order. Step 1 routes; step 2 computes; step 3 gates. None of the three is a carrier type you can classify a brief into.

**1. Find the current affect carrier — and route on the answer.** Content, covers, the game world, food, the physical object. This step decides *where the affect comes from*, not whether the interface may have any.
RU: сначала найди нынешнего носителя аффекта — это вопрос маршрута, а не приговор.
- **A carrier exists.** The interface does not *manufacture* affect and does not top up what is already delivered — that is what delegation means. What it may still do is **propagate the carrier's own sign**: extract the sign from the product or the business's mythology, primitivise it, and run it through the layout as one system. See "Where sign comes from" above; that is the whole of the interface's legitimate atemporal work on such a brief, and it is not surplus stylisation. Stylisation that adds affect *of its own* against the existing carrier is the loss.
- **Slot 2 says "nobody".** The interface is the carrier. The return on an atemporal device is then real and is computed at step 2 against its cost.

**2. Compute both sides. Do not read a verdict off a carrier name.**
- **Cost** = the device's per-pass charge toll × the pass count over the contact window (slot 4).
- **Return** = the affect delivered where slot 2 named the carrier "nobody". Where slot 2 already names a carrier, the return on *added* affect is zero by definition and no pass count is needed — the propagation route in step 1 is the only branch left.
- State the sign of cost minus return, out loud, with both inputs named.
RU: цена атемпорального приёма = плата за проход × число проходов; отдача считается только там, где носителя аффекта нет.

Three instances of this computation, kept because each of them inverts something, not because they are categories to sort into: a surface present every session at daily contact multiplies a small toll by an unbounded pass count, so the loss compounds while the gain does not; a carrier the user *arrives at* rather than works through is met once or rarely, so the same device is a one-time asset and the budget should go there; a hot repeated path charges the toll at the frequency of the path, and the quality of the gesture does not enter the arithmetic at all. Two briefs with the same carrier type and different contact frequencies get opposite verdicts here — that is what the computation is for, and a table of carrier types cannot produce it.

**3. Separability is the gate, and it has a precondition.** A device that eats the separability of blocks — glow, blur, a soft shadow standing in for a boundary — is cancelled outright, not reduced, **wherever a group-membership error is possible on this carrier**. That condition is a property of slot 5, not of the carrier class: where the user must read which block a datum belongs to, separability is not tradeable against impression and no return at step 2 buys it back.
RU: приём, съедающий отделимость блоков, отменяется целиком — там, где ошибка принадлежности возможна.
- **Counter-case, and it is real:** a one-pass carrier with no membership decision to get wrong — a mark, a frame read at a glance, a surface where nothing has to be attributed to anything — has no separability to protect, and a boundary-dissolving surface is then a legal derivation rather than a prohibited look. Name the membership decision that exists before you invoke this gate; if you cannot name one, the gate does not fire.

This is why the reading is local. One product legitimately carries an atemporal verdict on its dedicated carrier and the opposite verdict on its hot path; that divergence is the SEGMENTS line, not an inconsistency.

## The unresolved contradiction: remove the logo

The test exists — remove the mark and the product name and ask whose brand this is, or substitute a different business under the same carrier and see what survives.
RU: убрать логотип и подставить другой бизнес — что переживёт подстановку.

The author uses it in both directions and does not reconcile them. In one place it denies plot graphics their claimed recognisability: recognisability was delegated to the mark, so the graphics were carrying nothing. In another he argues the reverse, that the sign-loaded plot is exactly what does not survive substitution while typeface and palette do.

Do not resolve this into a rule. Run the test, report which of the two readings your result supports, and treat the verdict as a hypothesis with the observation that would settle it named — per the unanswered-gate rule in SKILL.md. Anyone quoting this test as settled method is over-claiming; see `references/limits.md`.

## Exit condition for the sign column

Every slot has a named strategy — high, low, or delegated outside the row of blocks — and every high-load slot names its interpretants for the named audience. Judged at both scales, screen and block: expect the two verdicts to differ, and let the block's function in the scenario resolve them rather than defaulting to either.
