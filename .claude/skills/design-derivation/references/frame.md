# Value and Modus — what the thing is measured by

> Loaded when: Filling Ledger slots 1–3 and 5–6; the brief is fresh or the frame is contested; a lower gate forces the frame to be re-opened.

This file produces the answers a charge column cannot be computed without: what this thing is measured by, who came here for what, what a block even is here, which contour each piece of information sits in, and whether advance is live. It settles nothing about form. If you find yourself deciding a form question here, you have left this file.

## The level correction, before anything else

Three levels are routinely collapsed into one word. Keep them apart or every downstream verdict inherits the confusion.

- **Modus** is the designer's style of thinking — a cell in a 3×3 matrix, vertical (temporal) axis × horizontal (topical) axis, three positions each. It types a person and a task, not a layout.
  RU: модус — стиль мышления дизайнера, клетка матрицы 3×3 по вертикальной (темпоральной) и горизонтальной (топической) осям.
- **Defining properties** are the properties by which a designer's modus is identified. They are not a checklist for an artefact.
  RU: облигатные свойства — свойства, по которым определяется модус дизайнера, а не требования к макету.
- **Paradigm** is the artefact level: the decision this brief takes about this thing.
  RU: парадигма — артефактный уровень, решение по конкретной работе.

The levers you actually pull sit at the artefact level: background, typeface, information density, share of area given to image, admissibility of stylisation. Derive each from the reading you wrote in the Ledger; none of them is a defining property of a modus and none is settled in advance.
RU: рычаги артефактного уровня — фон, гарнитура, плотность, доля площади под изображение, допустимость стилизации; они выводятся, а не задаются.

Struck: "a small image with text inside an atemporal block is an error by definition". Content-in-absolute has an upper bound too, and the beginner's error runs in both directions. Do not carry a definitional error where a derivation is owed.

### Typing a designer is not reading a work

Reading a work gives two independent scores (Ledger slots 1–2) which may diverge on the same layout — weak temporal with strong atemporal, and the reverse.
RU: чтение работы — две независимые шкалы одного макета, способные расходиться.

Typing is a separate object: a task has a coordinate on the matrix, a designer applies a vector, and the mismatch between them is the diagnosis — about the designer, not about the work. Modus 5 is the dual centre: the task is still decomposed and the method not yet chosen. Do not let a typing statement substitute for a reading, and never write a typing sentence into a Ledger slot.
RU: задача имеет координату на матрице, дизайнер прикладывает вектор; несовпадение и есть диагноз — о дизайнере.

The industry or the team applies a vector too, and it carries a designer into a foreign modus regardless of intent — the corpus term is drift. Name it when you catch a decision arriving from a channel rather than from this brief.
RU: дрейф — течение индустрии или коллектива, сносящее дизайнера в чужой модус.

### One worked instance, not a rule

A daily-work CMS drawn on a dark background with light text: the goal-setting is temporal, reading on dark is slower, so a measurable indicator drops against the same drawing on light. That is a derivation from the temporal reading, not a fact about backgrounds. Counter-case: on a carrier where dwelling is the instrumentalised behaviour, or where the affect carrier is the content shown against that background, the same choice is derived positively. The number of hours the surface is on screen decides, not the colour.
RU: модус диктует рычаги: фон — не вопрос вкуса там, где время инструментализовано; но тот же фон выводится в плюс, когда носителем аффекта является контент.

## Value: who came here for what

Order of derivation, and it does not commute:

1. Name the value — what this product exists to hand over.
2. Derive the functionality set from the value; strike what does not approach it.
3. Only then order the blocks: what is first by accent, what is second, what is third.

RU: ценность → функционал → секвенция; порядок акцентов выводится последним.

Checks that fire here:

- Does the highest-charged block coincide with the value carrier? A mismatch found here is cheaper than a mismatch found in the charge column.
- Size is spent on what the user came to learn, not on what they already know. A screen reached by tapping one's own icon does not owe the largest type to one's own name.
  RU: размер отдают тому, за чем пришли, а не тому, что уже известно.
- Count the blocks the scenario makes obligatory, as an integer, and carry that integer into Ledger slot 5. It is the input to the required span, and it is the only honest defence against "this is a complex product".
- Where no brief exists, the question to answer is not "who is the audience" but "what is the value and which value is shown first". Several equally good candidates for second place are legitimate — mark them as hypotheses about the block ordering, do not resolve them by taste.
- An alternative scenario that looks plausible: state it out loud as an opponent would and try to break it. In practice the surviving alternative is more often built into the solution than used to dismantle it, so treat this as a widening move, not a filter.
  RU: академическая щедрость — допущение более широкого случая, чем буква задания; чаще встраивается в решение, чем разваливает его.

## The brief: what to extract, and what not to add

- A number in the brief that looks arbitrary usually encodes a physical constraint the author cannot see — capacity, throughput, a licence. Ask what constraint could have produced it; with no answer, treat the constraint as hidden and do not expand the range. Liberalise only where hidden nuance is impossible by construction.
  RU: за цифрой в задании ищи скрытое физическое ограничение; нет объяснения — считай ограничение скрытым и не расширяй.
- Do not liberalise a spec where an implicit design system may be hidden: the unstated knowledge about which block orderings bring money is not readable from a layout or a UI kit, and a newcomer reconstructs it wrongly.
  RU: имплицитная дизайн-система — негласное знание о приоритетах, не считываемое из макета.
- Lay out the bare logos — text blocks with no background, no outline, no styling — before drawing anything, and look at where the accent asks to go on its own.
  RU: разложить голые логосы до рисовки.
- A definition given as a list of features derives nothing; refuse it and ask for the criterion. Full protocol in `references/argument.md`.

Two moves belong here by topic but rest on a single occurrence each, and are quarantined below rather than used as method: reading the brief as a declaration of modus, and deriving scenario priority from the company's impetus.

## Carrier and audience as constraints on the frame

### Carrier

Ask for the physical carrier and its diagonal before deciding anything about size, and relate dimensions to a person standing at their actual distance rather than to the screen. This applies to physical screens — a kiosk, a wall panel, a small handheld (52″ / 32″ / 8″ are illustrations of the range, not a set of cases). The effect where something too large at close range falls out of the field of view is one the author himself calls uncategorised; use it as a hypothesis, never as a verdict.
RU: носитель и диагональ — соотносить размеры с человеком вплотную, а не с экраном; эффект «слишком крупное вблизи» автор сам называет некатегоризованным.

Porting a pattern from one device to another: ask which resource is in surplus on the target — width on a desktop, height on a handheld — and let the surplus, not the source layout, set the arrangement.

For everything the carrier decides about blocks, block ordering, renewal events and advance, go to `references/carriers.md` the moment the carrier is not a plain screen.

### Addressee

The addressee sets the mode of presentation, and the covering note that came with the work usually reveals which modus it was made in. Strike blocks that are empty for the named addressee.
RU: адресат определяет модус подачи; из сопроводиловки виден модус, в котором сделана работа.

The five-part addressee taxonomy that circulated in earlier reconstructions is cut — do not name addressee types from a list; name this one.

### Audience competence — corrected

"Competence as a budget" is not the author's framing and must not be used as one. His operative stance is the opposite in direction: assume by default that the user will not work anything out, in order to land inside the smallest number of clichés at the largest audience. Count the audience's clichés, not your own.
RU: считать клише аудитории, а не свои; по умолчанию не рассчитывать на догадливость юзера.

The half that converts into arithmetic and is used downstream: every element that pulls accent costs one logical operation, and a cliché element does not count as one. That gives you a countable load for the layout instead of an impression of busyness.
RU: каждый оттягивающий акцент элемент — одна логическая операция; клише за операцию не считается.

Audience width also sets how far a departure from a mass solution may go; the criterion for taking or refusing a mass solution is in `references/operations.md` and is explicitly still open.

## Delegated affect, imperativeness, contact frequency

Before requiring atmosphere from an interface, find who already carries it — the content, the covers, the game world, the food, the music. If a carrier exists, the atemporal is delegated to it and the interface does not top it up; a neutral interface is then legitimate, and affect manufactured against the existing carrier is a straight loss. What remains open on such a brief is propagation — carrying the existing carrier's sign through the layout — which is a different operation and is derived in `references/sign.md`, not here.
RU: найди нынешнего носителя аффекта; если он есть, атемпораль делегирована ему, и интерфейс её не добирает.

Ask separately whether the interface is imperative or voluntary, and how often contact happens. The two combine: an imperative product at daily contact amnesties costs paid once during learning and never amnesties costs paid on every pass. The full pair of lists is in `references/frame.md`; the criterion — once at learning versus every pass — is what you need here to fill slots 1 and 5.
RU: императивность амнистирует издержки, оплачиваемые один раз при обучении, и не амнистирует издержки каждого прохода.

Test the temporal reading in the inverted direction: do not ask whether slowing would spoil it — ask whether **acceleration** would break the atemporal. Pole A is the instrumentalisation of time in either direction, deliberate dwelling included.
RU: проверять не «испортится ли от замедления», а не сломает ли ускорение атемпораль.

Any claim about time owes a measurement artefact — time on task, step count, a usability report, an eye-tracker. Without one the temporal claim is rhetoric; the corpus name for that is fake method.
RU: заявлено время — потребуй артефакт замера; нет артефакта — схематоз.

## Contours

Three contours, by depth of access: read without any action; the second adds block orderings; the third replaces them. The second contour zeroes the current block ordering and spawns a new one, which is what makes it safe to load with detail and with atemporal material.
RU: контуры 1/2/3 — считывается без действия; секвенции добавляются; секвенции заменяются.

The distribution criterion is the **degree of importance of the information** — primary, secondary, tertiary — not the frequency with which a parameter is needed or changes. That correction matters: frequency and importance diverge exactly where the expensive mistakes are.
RU: распределение по контурам — по степени важности информации (перво-, второ-, третьестепенная).

- Depth is inverse to criticality. Data whose omission decides the outcome belongs in the first contour, however rarely it is consulted. Module positions are learned calmly and used under stress, so a stressed path may not be the one that taught the layout.
  RU: критичность задаёт контур: чем выше цена пропуска, тем ближе к контуру «смотри».
- Contour membership decides which block ordering an element belongs to: second-contour elements sit on a different ordering and must not be charged against first-contour neighbours. Carry this into the charge column (`references/charges.md`).
- A block that requires a click to reveal its content: ask whether the click is obligatory for the meaning. If it is not, the click is both non-obvious and extra — hover, automatic display, or a split into two blocks are the alternatives to weigh.
- Pushing reference material out of the first contour is one of the few ways to reduce the obligatory block count without contesting the brief. It is an operation on accent, so it is signed by the Ledger like any other (`references/operations.md`).

## Advance

Advance is how much more time a viewer or user is willing to allocate to this carrier. It is a credit, issued by impression and by relevant output, spent by every repeat and every peering.
RU: аванс — сколько времени зритель готов ещё выделить носителю; выдаётся впечатлением и релевантной выдачей, тратится на повторах.

**The reading.** Walk the same block ordering a second time, joint by joint, and at each joint ask one question: does this block add credit — impression, delivered value, relevant output, an obstacle removed — or burn it — a repeat, self-similarity, a superfluous accent, an unexplained fork. Over the whole carrier it must add more than it spends. This is a second reading over one ordering, not a verdict at each seam.
RU: пройти носитель поблочно и на каждом стыке спросить: докидывает блок аванс или сжигает; докидывать надо больше, чем тратить.

**Calibration by entry point.** Advance is never assessed absolutely. Cold traffic arrives with almost none; a returning user arrives with a lot; a deep link lands mid-ordering with whatever the referrer already spent. Permissible aggression — a modal, an extra step, a long read — follows from that number and from nothing else. The same modal is tolerated on high advance and produces the exit on zero.
RU: оценивать не абсолютно, а по точке входа: холодный трафик — почти нулевой аванс, возврат — высокий; отсюда допустимая агрессия.

**The off switch.** Under absolute temporality — the user has already decided and there will be no drop — the advance question switches off entirely. Write OFF in the slot and say why out loud; an advance argument made where advance does not apply is the frame arguing with itself.
RU: при абсолютной темпоральности вопрос аванса выключается.

**What advance, and only advance, settles.** Four decisions have no other honest source:

- how many cards and how many screens the thing gets;
- whether an onboarding step is warranted — plot the credit across the steps: the step dips first, and it pays for itself only if each later step gains and the whole run finishes with a margin;
- whether an image comes out of the layout;
- whether a control is hidden on a repeat visit.

RU: аванс решает число карточек и экранов, нужен ли онбординг, убирать ли картинку, прятать ли контрол при повторном заходе.

**Instruments and non-instruments.** The scroll-percentage instrument — "at what percentage of scroll the first impression appears, everything above it counts as unread" — is not his and is not to be used. His actual instrument is qualitative: the point at which he himself dropped off, named as a specific block. A row of similar items collapses into one unit and devalues around the fourth or fifth element (illustration, not a threshold), which is why text on the later cards of a long row is written for no one; take that to `references/charges.md` rather than settling it here.

## Exit condition

Frame and scenario are a loop, not a sequence: the paradigm sets how a block is read, while the temporal reading is derived from the scenario of the specific segment. Iterate until the frame stops rewriting the scenario reading and the scenario stops rewriting the frame. Then the file has produced Ledger slots 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 and the block half of slot 4.
RU: рамка и сценарий определяются взаимно; парадигма задаёт трактовку блока, темпоральность выводится из сценария конкретного сегмента.

If a slot cannot be answered from the brief, do not fill it from habit. Ask, or mark the line a hypothesis and name the observation that would settle it.

## Quarantined here: single-occurrence material

Each of the following occurs once in the corpus. Usable as a hypothesis to raise with a user, never as method, never as a defence. Full register in `references/limits.md`.

- Company impetus — what the company takes the market with — as the source of scenario priority before opening a layout. SINGLE.
- Reading the brief as a declaration of modus (a phrase about who warms the client up implying that affect must be **removed** from the design rather than added). SINGLE.
- The imaginary product manager as a device for producing a value statement without a brief. SINGLE.
- Demanding a named hole in the product plus a metric before any layout is shown. SINGLE.
- The substitution question — why this, when the channel already exists. SINGLE.
- Qualifying an abstract, scenario-free question as context-free reasoning. SINGLE.

## Never reinstate, frame side

- Naming the niche without reading the description and testing the guess on the people present — absent from the corpus; the practice is the reverse, the description and the brief are read first.
- The five-part addressee taxonomy.
- "Prolegomena" as a requirement for how work is presented.
- The scroll-percentage instrument for advance.
- The five-seconds-of-slowdown test for the temporal axis, and "would it spoil if the person spent twice as long".
- "Have you often seen screenshots like this" as a test of invented value.
- Running every outcome and hunting for a missing refusal screen.
