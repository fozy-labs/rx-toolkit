# Limits and Forks

> Loaded when: About to state a number, cite this model as authority against a user or a stakeholder, attribute a formulation to the author, close one of the open tensions, or answer a question about where this comes from.

## Provenance

This skill encodes one reconstructed reasoning model, not a body of design knowledge. It was reconstructed from roughly 134 hours of publicly published Russian-language lectures and live design-critique streams by a single author (YouTube channel "Эрзац" / "Дазайн", @ersatzdasein), then put through an adversarial verification pass against a corpus of 63 transcripts. It was assembled without his involvement. He has not reviewed it, endorsed it, or authorised it; it is not official and not a distillation he sanctioned. The verification cut it substantially: fewer than four in ten of the extracted moves survived unchanged, roughly a quarter rest on a single occurrence and are quarantined in the register below, and several were removed as inverted or absent from the corpus. Where this skill and the author disagree, the author is not bound by this skill.

This file is the authority on all of that. Where it and any other file in this skill disagree about a status, an attribution, or a number, this file wins.

## How to use this file

Three operations, and nothing else.

1. **Check status before asserting.** A claim's verification status decides what you may do with it: CONFIRMED closes an argument; core-confirmed-but-narrowed closes it only inside the narrowed scope, and you state the scope; SINGLE may seed a hypothesis and must carry the observation that would settle it; cut material may not be used at all.
2. **Present an open fork as a fork.** Four of the thirteen forks are not closed by the corpus. On those, name both branches to the user, say which one you took and on what evidence, and never let taste pass as the model's ruling.
RU: незакрытая развилка предъявляется как развилка, а не как вердикт модели.
3. **Never cite this skill as authority against a person.** It is a reconstruction with a measured survival rate. Against a stakeholder, argue from the derivation you just performed on this brief, not from the pedigree of the model.

## Verification arithmetic

Counts below describe the verification pass, not any design quantity.

- 125 extracted moves: 47 hold unchanged, 35 hold at the core but were claimed more broadly than the corpus supports, 34 rest on a single occurrence, 9 were cut as inverted or non-existent.
- 7 axes: 3 confirmed, 3 narrowed, 1 inverted outright.
- 13 forks: 9 close on a criterion the author states himself, 4 stay open.

Read the last line as the substantive finding: most of the apparent self-contradiction was an artefact of merging different contexts, not the author disagreeing with himself.
RU: большая часть противоречий — след сведения разных контекстов, а не расхождение автора с собой.

What survived most solidly: accent and charge, block ordering, hierarchy, the renewal law, advance, the sufficiency principle, contours of interaction, parasitic density, and the ought/is pairing. These work in lectures, work in live critique, and actually change an edit.
RU: акцент и заряд, секвенция, иерархичность, закон обновления, аванс, принцип достаточности, контуры взаимодействия, паразитарная плотность, должное/сущее.

## The four forks that stay open

Say out loud that these are open when you touch them. Closing one silently is how a reconstruction turns into doctrine.

**T4 — where the spacing progression may legitimately be broken.** The progression and the three grounds are confirmed. The size of the admissible departure is not: the author declared the tolerance zone for exaggeration undefined. So: name the ground, count by eye, and mark the magnitude as your call rather than the model's.
RU: основание нарушения назвать обязательно, величину отступления объявить своим решением, не выводом модели.

**T9 — the mass-solution criterion.** Reconstructed from a mechanism, not from a stated rule. The find-versus-intercept split holds, and so do two modifiers: audience width sets the permitted range, and a departure from a habitual layout is paid for with a new logic you can articulate. What is *not* settled is where the line falls for a mixed element that must be both found and noticed. Mark that case as a hypothesis.
RU: развилка «найти» / «перехватить» держится; смешанный случай не решён.

**T10 — the drift of the term for block ordering.** The author declares a strict sense and does not himself observe it. Consequence for you: write the term strictly, and when reading the corpus never build an inference about repeatability on the word alone.
RU: термин писать строго; при чтении корпуса не строить на нём вывода о повторяемости.

**T13-A — the designer's role.** Whether someone is still a designer he decides by drawing (the work is inalienable); what level they are at, by whether a derivable model is present. A third frame about roles is absent from the corpus, and the studio-versus-product formula belongs to an invited mentor. Do not fuse these into one ladder, and do not use any of them to grade a person.
RU: вопросы «дизайнер ли» и «какого уровня» не смешивать; третьей рамки в корпусе нет.

## Single-occurrence register

Every item below rests on one occurrence in the corpus. Admissible as a hypothesis that names its own falsifier. Not admissible as method, not as an argument to a user, not as a reason for an edit. Nothing here may be restated in `SKILL.md` or in any other reference file.

**Frame and value.** Company impetus as a frame. A brief read as a declaration of modus. The imagined product manager. Demanding a named hole and a metric before any layout. The substitution question ("why this, when a messenger exists"). Classifying an abstract question as context-free reasoning.

**Scenario.** A checking cadence of 3–5 seconds against 15 seconds (forbidden as a transferable number — see the numbers register). Decomposing an indirect metric into a chain of direct ones (one video, one industry).

**Charge and block ordering — six consecutive moves from a single fifteen-minute teaching video, and they must be quarantined together.** Describing one interval in words. Silhouette approximation. Minimum-edits. **Reverse translation and its reversibility check.** Semantics before separation. Weight taken from the neighbour in meaning. Also single, from nearby material: nested block orderings; multiplying probabilities along a chain of cards; a memorability requirement used as rejection; confronting an entire block ordering at once; the logarithmic return on type size; inversion of the accent colour.

**Sign-load.** Showing a characteristic through a staged scene instead of stating it. Detail defined as form boundaries, run down to 16 px. An object plus a characteristic not proper to it. Leaving a completion step for the viewer (he presents this as new and unsettled thinking). Not extending a stylistic device across everything, and keeping lore signs off the combat screen — two separate thoughts an extractor glued into one move.

**Surface and composition.** Assembling a block starting from the visual (with his own caveat that information projects have no visual). Non-spatial means of separation (the whole passage comes from one conversation with a guest). **Membership fixed before distance.** A key-value pair sharing one baseline. Affordance as a position on a bluntness scale. Setting typography from the most complex screen downwards.

**Self-check and defence.** Reflecting on your own gaze trajectory (the training period he names is a month). Demanding an interception mechanism whenever something is removed. Going one level up. Ranking an unclassifiable layout below bad informing. Reconstructing your own version of a block after the fact. Polishing paying for itself against escapism (the word occurs once in the whole corpus).

## Numbers register

No number leaves this skill without its status attached at the point of use. This register is the authority on which status each one carries.

**Illustrations — a proportion the author reached for once to make a point visible; never a target.** 90/10, 75/20/5, 70/30 as charge splits. The banner triad's floating 80/15/5 and 60/30/10 — the *order* attract → inform → lead away is confirmed, the proportions drift between tellings. A 2–4× ratio between neighbouring charges. The 1–10 charge scale itself (an arbitrary ruler; only spans and pit depths on it mean anything). Five to seven scenario-obligatory blocks as the working threshold above which a wide span stops being reachable. Accuracy at about a fifth of the assessment rather than a half.

**Norm with bounds the author himself left undefined.** The spacing progression multiplier of about 1.6–1.8 along letter → space → line → paragraph → block. Use it as the shape of a progression you rebuild for this brief, and state that the tolerance for exaggeration is undefined.

**Confirmed limits.** Two levels of card nesting, past which the eye stops distinguishing steps. No more than three swipe points.

**Forbidden — do not emit these anywhere, in any file, under any framing.**
- Multiples of four and eight for spacing: a guest's criterion, which the author disputes.
- Fifteen steps reduced to three.
- Six swipe points (the corpus says no more than three).
- An accent step of 1.5–1.8, imported from the spacing progression — a different multiplier, a different operation.
- A checking cadence of 3–5 seconds generalised beyond one combat game interface.
- A type-size run-down to 16 px.

## Never reinstate

Cut for cause. Each line gives the cause, because a cut without its reason grows back.

**Inverted — the poles or the mechanism were reversed.**
- The opposition between block ordering and "track". The author publicly retracted the distinction and merged both into one term; historically the poles were the other way round. The repeatability criterion he rejected with it goes too.
- "A graph instead of an arrow." In live critique he demands arrows in flows and criticises their absence.
- "Accent is not a property of a block." His definition says literally the opposite.
- "Remove the text — does the plot survive." The experiment exists; his conclusion is the reverse — the text, the logos, is primary, and without it an envelope of surplus densities remains. The surviving half is a separate claim: abstraction is not sign-load, perception reaches for sign-load and has no time.
- Layers and otherness. His mechanism is self-similarity and merging densities, and the conclusion that an interface outline returns an element to its own layer contradicts his practice.
- "Not a multiple of two." His own step permits two explicitly.
- "The block everyone redoes is under-defined in meaning." Inverted: a shared complaint about one block is good news to him (the error is localised); the bad case is everyone disliking everything a little. The intervention-depth ladder attached to it is absent.
- "Symmetry lowers self-similarity." For him symmetry is itself a self-similar structure; the load comes from breaking it.

**Absent — the extractor built it; the corpus has nothing.**
- Calibrating detail on a hyperdetail axis.
- Interrogating Fitts, Hick, or gestalt. Those names do not occur in the corpus at all, so the model has no position on them. Do not manufacture one.
- Naming the niche without reading the brief; almost always he reads the description and the brief first.
- Running outcomes and hunting for a refusal screen.
- "Have you often seen screenshots like this."
- The five-part addressee taxonomy (shop / buyer / user / manager / developer).
- Pulling a percentage-of-scroll as an instrument for advance. His instrument is qualitative: his own point of drop-off on a specific block.
- The five-seconds-of-slowdown test for the temporal reading, and "would it get worse at twice the time".
- The claim that a fact, a metric, and an authority can be fudged but a chain of derivation cannot. The real marker is non-attributability and unverifiable provenance.
- Naming a form with a word; sign-load inflation.
- "Пролегомены" as a requirement on a presentation (in the corpus it means only an introduction to a theory).
- "The user will just cut this block off as junk" as a self-check; it appears once, as a weapon against someone else's argument.

- Interfaces also get printed; the left third of the colour box is lost; cyan is the cheat colour — a guest.

## This skill's own scaffolding, declared

Everything in this section is the skill's construction, not the author's practice. Say so if asked.

- **There is no six-stage pipeline.** No position in it is confirmed as an obligatory beat. The gate order is the corrected dependency skeleton verification reconstructed — a priority order plus two loops.
- **Framing is not the first step, and epistemic hygiene is not the last.** In the overwhelming majority of live critiques he enters from the surface and builds the frame retroactively. Only two moves chronologically precede drawing: laying out the bare logos, and not liberalising where an implicit design system may be hidden.
- **Operational tests read cleaner here than in the corpus.** Roughly half of each test is a plausible reconstruction laid over one real move. Treat a test's *shape* as the skill's, its *core* as his.
- **The Ledger, the gate discipline, the size router, and the emitted FLIP / SEGMENTS / INAPPLICABLE / DIVERGENCE lines are this skill's operationalisation.** He writes none of them.
- **The charge curve is optional by his own words** — he calls building it an indulgence and never builds one in a live critique. The measurement unit is confirmed; the ritual is not.
- **The topical position is his own confirmed term, but promoting it to a Ledger slot is slightly stronger than the corpus supports.** It is absent from the judging rubric he takes part in, and in live critique it surfaces mainly through its derivatives — contours, block orderings, "could not complete the scenario".
- **The verification pass covered the axes, the moves in the six stages, and the tensions — and nothing else.** Two bodies of material in this skill therefore carry no individual verification standing, and both are heavily used: the **anti-pattern entries** in `references/anti-patterns.md`, whose Status lines record the standing of the underlying move and not of the entry; and the **worked cases** in `references/derivations.md`, which come from the extraction layer and were not re-verified case by case. Their mechanisms are mostly traceable; their composition into entries and cases is this skill's.
- **The per-carrier derivations in `references/carriers.md` are this skill's composition** of confirmed mechanisms (renewal events, pass count, the demarcation by carrier) into a procedure. The only carrier-specific claim the corpus confirms outright is the banner triad's *order*; its proportions drift, and everything the corpus says about print belongs to a guest.
- **The two-level nesting limit is confirmed for cards.** Its extension to substrates, plates and other layer types is this skill's analogy, and the files that use it say so.

## Boundary of applicability

Outside these bounds the model has no verdict, and neither do you. Saying "the model does not reach here" is a legitimate output; dressing a preference in this vocabulary is not.

**Carrier.** The model reads what is drawn as a layout or a graphic and consumed by the eye inside a frame. Objects, speech, process, hiring, organisational design are outside it. He marks his own excursions there as hunch or joke; mark yours the same way or stay silent.
RU: демаркация по носителю — рисуется как макет или графика и потребляется взглядом в кадре.

**Physical size.** Carrier-and-diagonal reasoning is confirmed for physical screens only — kiosk, 52", 8", 32". The effect whereby something too large at close range goes unnoticed he calls uncategorised himself, so do not derive from it.

**Advance switches off.** Under absolute temporality — the user has already decided, there will be no drop — the advance question does not apply. Write the OFF and say why.
RU: при абсолютной темпоральности вопрос аванса выключается.

**Sign-load without a named audience is undefined.** The audience is inside the definition: how many interpretants *these* people read unambiguously and what response they give. No audience named, no sign verdict.

**Charge without its limiters degenerates.** Three things stop the span axis collapsing into "more disproportion is better": at the stage of choosing a scenario low hierarchy is normal and the span belongs inside the chosen block; hierarchy competes with the transmission of sign; and a step can be excessive. Quote none of the axis without them.

**Refutation takes a series.** A model is refuted by a series of failed variants, not by one failure. One bad outcome is not evidence against a derivation, and one good outcome is not evidence for it.

**Jargon stays inside the model.** These terms belong in the derivation and in critique among people who share them. They do not belong in interface text, and they do not belong in a message to a stakeholder who has not agreed to them.

**Not covered at all, and no vocabulary here reaches them:** markup and component code, framework and library choice, copywriting, data-chart and visualisation specification, accessibility conformance. The corpus grounds no verdict in those areas.
