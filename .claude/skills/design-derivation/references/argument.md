# Layer 0 — what counts as an argument, and how a decision is defended

> Loaded when: A claim is made, defended, or received — yours, the client's, or a critic's; or a metric, an authority, a borrowed law, or a soft word appears; or a decision must be defended to a stakeholder.

This layer is not a design step. It is a standing filter over every other gate, and it is applied first of all to **someone else's** justification — including your own from an hour ago. Until it is settled what counts as an argument here, no verdict on the layout is available.
RU: пока не решено, что считается аргументом, судить нельзя; слой применяется прежде всего к чужим обоснованиям.

On a fresh brief it produces no written output. It produces one written line every time a claim is made, defended, or received.

## The seven filters

Run whichever fires. Each names what the claim owes; a claim that cannot pay is not refuted, it is **inadmissible**, which is a different and stronger thing.

### 1. Time claim

Any assertion that a decision costs or saves time owes a **named measurement artefact**: time on task, step count, a usability report, an eye-tracker. No artefact, no claim.
RU: заявлено время — назови конкретный замер или не произноси утверждения.

The corpus name for a time claim without its artefact is fake method (схематоз). It applies symmetrically: to "fewer densities, therefore faster", to "this flow is quicker", and to your own edit note. The honest fallback is to relabel: if the change cannot alter group membership, contour assignment, or the accent palette, it is **accuracy**, it goes on the atemporal account, and it stops being a time claim at all.

### 2. Black box

"A big company does it", "users are used to it", "it's convenient", "it was signed off", "it works". None is an argument; each closes the question instead of answering it.
RU: защита удобством или согласованиями — апелляция к чёрному ящику.

The specific trap: **any workable layout lets the scenario complete**, so completion proves nothing about what would have worked better. Ask for the derivation. Where none exists, say that none exists rather than producing a counter-authority.

### 3. Soft word

Trust, irritation, convenience, consistency, cleanliness, balance. Operationalise into something that changes a decision, or drop it as a reified word.
RU: мягкое понятие либо операционализируется, либо выбрасывается как реифицированное.

**Test it at its limit.** Push the criterion to full compliance and look at what it produces there. Beauty as balance of the composition, taken literally, means equal charges everywhere — which is the flat gradient the same critic would reject, so the effect they admire came from the disproportion the criterion forbids. A criterion whose limiting case yields nothing is not a criterion.

The test protects as well as destroys: run it *before* discarding a soft word, because some of them operationalise cleanly and then become usable.

### 4. Metric — the T2 procedure

The fork "metrics are impossible / metrics are obligatory" is closed by three filters, applied in order.

1. **Type of claim.** A claim about time demands a measurement. A claim that is not operationalisable does not get a metric invented for it.
2. **Statistical power and attribution.** Rejecting an A/B test for insufficient traffic or for unattributable results is not rejecting measurement as a method. When struck with a number, ask for **attribution** (what else changed) and **provenance** (who, how, on what sample).
3. **Role in the argument.** A metric is legitimate as the *source* of a task and as its *target*. It is illegitimate as the *closing move*.
RU: метрика законна как источник задачи и цель, незаконна как закрывающий аргумент.

Where verification is genuinely unavailable, the fallback is **consistency with decisions the system has already made** — and it is a real argument, not a shrug, because an inconsistent system charges the user a rule-learning cost on every screen. Usability tests and corridor checks are the author's own prescription where traffic is short.

**Refutation takes a series.** A model is refuted by a series of failed variants, never by one. One bad outcome is not evidence against a derivation and one good outcome is not evidence for it.
RU: модель опровергает провал серии вариантов, а не один провал.

Do not carry the claim that facts and metrics can be fudged while a chain of derivation cannot — it is not in the corpus and the real marker is different: **non-attributability and unverifiable provenance**. See `references/limits.md`.

### 5. Analogy and borrowed law — the T11 procedure

Cross-domain transfer is a source of hypotheses and **never a link in a derivation**.
RU: аналогия — источник гипотезы, не звено вывода.

Two filters, and together they show there is no double standard here, only one standard with marked exits.

- **Register.** An analogy may stay in the reasoning and may be said out loud as a hunch or a joke. It may not be cited in the verdict. If it is doing load-bearing work, strike it and re-derive from inside the model.
- **Demarcation by carrier.** The model reaches what is drawn as a layout or a graphic and consumed by the eye inside a frame. Objects, speech, process, hiring, organisational design are outside it. Excursions there are marked as hunch or kept silent.

**Extending a concept obliges you to name the class where it stops working.** This is compulsory, not a courtesy. A model that answers both "how does this interface work" and "how does a person walk down a corridor" answers neither.

Do not manufacture a position on named laws of perception. Those names do not occur in the corpus at all, so the model has no verdict on them; inventing one is the same failure in the opposite direction.

### 6. Post-hoc — hermeneutics against derivation

Ask whether a model produced the form, or the description was fitted to the form afterwards.
RU: герменевтика продаёт решение и не порождает формы.

Hermeneutics is not useless — it gives a decision weight when a project is handed over — but it is a rhetorical frame, not a model, and the discipline is to call it by its name. Its operational consequence is the discard rule: a Ledger written to justify a drawing that already exists is layout hermeneutics; discard it and re-derive, never edit it into shape.

The legal form of the same move: retroactive frame-building is fine as **entry**. In the overwhelming majority of live critique the reasoning starts from whatever first caught the eye and the frame is reconstructed afterwards. What is forbidden is leaving the frame silent and unfalsifiable — write it down, and let it be capable of overturning the drawing that prompted it.

### 7. Currency

The reading decides which defence is admissible for each element. **A defence paid in the wrong currency is void even when it sounds right** — an argument from affect offered for an element the reading defends by time, or the reverse.
RU: защита, оплаченная не той валютой, недействительна, даже если звучит убедительно.

Applied to a foreign term used inside its own field, the right verdict is usually "not applicable here", not "empty word". That distinction is the whole of the T10 fork below.

## Verdict discipline: what ought to be against what is — T13-B

Four steps, and the order does not commute.
RU: семантика задаёт должное, структура вычисляет сущее, вердикт — только расхождение, содержимое подключается последним.

1. **Name the value and what ought to be** — from slot 5, in semantics.
2. **Read what is off the form with the content ignored.** Structure computes this; you are reading charges, spans, orderings, membership, not meanings.
3. **State the verdict only as the divergence** between the two. There is no verdict that is not a divergence.
4. **Attach content last**, as a modifier of charge — colour and plot in the final step, never as the source of the reading.

**Self-check for the same failure.** Substitute different content of the same size into the layout. If the weighting is unchanged, the complaint is structural. If it changed, you are judging semantically and you owe the value you derive it from.
RU: подставь другое содержимое того же размера: развесовка не изменилась — претензия структурная.

**And the harder version of the same test.** If two objects share the properties your verdict claims to rest on and receive opposite verdicts, the verdict is not a function of those properties — it is impression narrated as assessment. State the interval, or the pair of blocks, that any verdict came from. A verdict without its interval is not a verdict.

## Derivability — the T-axis test on any rule, his or yours

A rule is admissible when a consequence follows from it **and** you can say where you would knowingly break it with a gain.
RU: годно то, из чего выводится следствие и видно, где его можно осознанно нарушить с выигрышем.

Four tests:

- **(a) Derivability.** Does anything follow? Can it be broken deliberately for a gain, and can you name the case?
- **(b) Contrastive variants (контрастные рисовки).** Draw the competing options and say why one wins. No answer means the choice was imprinting. The variant that inherits parameters already in use by neighbouring blocks wins, and it wins for a countable reason: it adds no visual rule.
- **(c) Black box.** Is this an appeal to something that cannot be opened?
- **(d) Definition by enumeration.** A definition given as a list of features derives nothing. Check it by **substitution**: if another of your own concepts fits the same list, the definition does not distinguish and must be refused.

**Binding correction.** Visual practice is not a vice in itself. A device is praised and adopted the moment a model-level reason for it can be given on the spot. The vice is practice unattached to *this* specificity and non-derivable. "Model" also does not mean "his model": an opponent with a real model is credited, and the objection is to over-generalisation.
RU: визуальная практика не порок сама по себе; порок — практика, не привязанная к специфике и невыводимая.

Do not gloss this axis with the reference-hoard term (соберуха). That word names a genre of content, not a property of a layout.

## Terminology — T10, and it is OPEN

Say out loud that this fork is open when you touch it.

One test for his terms and foreign ones alike: **something must be derivable from the term** — it must let you discard a hypothesis without research, shorten the path to a decision, or generate a prediction. A term from which nothing follows is not a term.
RU: из термина должно что-то выводиться; иначе это не термин.

Second filter: the **addressee**. Jargon belongs in the derivation and in critique among people who share it. It does not belong in interface text, and it does not belong in a message to a stakeholder who has not agreed to it.

What stays open: the author declares a strict sense for the block-ordering term and does not himself observe it. Consequence — write the term strictly, and when reading the corpus never build an inference about repeatability on the word alone.

## Applying the layer to yourself

The same filters, turned inward, and this is where the layer does most of its work.

- **Articulability.** Name the difference, say how it acts, and only then apply it. A decision that cannot be stated in the model's terms was picked up by practice; mark it and do not present it as an argument.
- **A fired intuition is unfinished, not disqualified.** Formalise it. What is rejected is the unreflected feeling, not the feeling. The claim that the eye outranks the model in a conflict is *not* supported by the corpus and runs against it — do not use it.
- **Do not judge from inside your own drawing.** Get the work out of your own frame; in that state the assessment is not critical. Wait for the questions that sound stupid.
- **Judge against value, not against the previous version.** Comparing before with after makes difference the criterion, and accumulated edits then look like development while being drift (дрейф).
- **Escalate when the defects are many and small.** While you are enumerating pixels, the level at which the work actually needs redoing is not visible. (Single-occurrence — usable as a prompt, not as a rule; see `references/limits.md`.)

## Defending a decision to a stakeholder

Three rules, and the first is the one this whole skill's provenance section exists to support.

1. **Never cite this model as authority.** It is a reconstruction with a measured survival rate, assembled without the author's involvement. Against a stakeholder you argue from the derivation you just performed on *this* brief — the Ledger line, the pair of blocks, the neighbour charged — and never from the pedigree of the model or the name of its author.
RU: не ссылайся на модель как на авторитет; ссылайся на вывод, проделанный на этом брифе.
2. **Translate out of the jargon.** Every term of art in this skill is replaceable by the observation it names: "this block recharges every time they read a paragraph, so they lose the thread once per paragraph", not "accent loop". A stakeholder who has not agreed to the vocabulary is owed the observation, not the word.
3. **Present an open fork as a fork.** Four of the thirteen tensions are not closed by the corpus. Name both branches, say which you took and on what evidence, and never let taste pass as the model's ruling.

**When you are struck with an argument rather than making one:** identify which of the seven filters it fails, say what the claim owes, and stop. Do not answer a black box with a counter-black-box, and do not answer a metric with a stronger metric you have not got. If the claim pays what it owes, it is a good claim and you concede it — that outcome is part of the layer working, not a failure of it.
