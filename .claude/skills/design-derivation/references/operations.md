# Operations — and why none of them has a sign

> Loaded when: About to add, remove, duplicate, stylise, iconify, divide, hide, de-energise, or substitute anything; or an edit is being defended; or a client's proposed change needs its sign checked.

## The rule this whole file exists to enforce

An operation on form has **no verdict attached to it**. It has a mechanism, and it has two readings — one under which it is a gain and one under which it is a loss. The Ledger picks the reading. Nothing else may.
RU: у операции нет знака: есть механизм и два прочтения; знак ставит строка Ledger, а не память о прошлом проекте.

The canonical demonstration, and the reason this file is separate from every other: **proliferating entities is a loss in an interface where time is instrumentalised and a gain on an atemporal game surface.** Identical act, opposite verdicts, and the thing that flipped it was slot 1, not the act.
RU: плодить сущности — вред в темпоральном интерфейсе и польза в атемпоральном игровом; поступок один, знак противоположный.

So when you catch yourself knowing what an operation is worth before you have read the slot, that is the failure this skill was built against, arriving in its most compact form. Recalling a sign is not faster than deriving one; it is a different activity that produces a remembered look.

## How to sign an operation — the procedure

Four steps, in order, and each one is written down.

1. **Name the operation** in the vocabulary below, not in the language of the outcome you want. "Make it cleaner" is not an operation; "remove two dividers and one substrate" is.
2. **Name the Ledger slot that governs it.** Every entry below names its governing slot. If the slot is empty, you are not entitled to the operation — go back to the gate.
3. **Read the sign off the slot**, in the slot's own terms: what does *this* line say the operation costs and what does it buy, here.
4. **Name the neighbour it charges.** Every operation redistributes; an operation with no named neighbour is arithmetic, not design.
RU: назвать операцию → назвать слот → снять знак со слота → назвать соседа, у которого отобран заряд.

**Falsifier, mandatory.** State what you would have to observe on this carrier for the sign to come out the other way. An operation signed with no statable inverse was not derived; it was recalled.

## The vocabulary

Each entry gives the mechanism, the reading under which it is a gain, the reading under which it is a loss, and the governing slot. None gives a verdict.

### Add an entity (block, layer, control, plot object)

Mechanism: an addition is a **redistribution of the whole accent palette**, not a "+1". The frame's budget is closed between cognitive acts, so the new entity takes weight silently from every existing one and the charges converge.
RU: добавление — переразложение палитры акцентов, а не прибавление элемента.
- Gain when: slot 2 names the affect carrier as "nobody" and the entity carries sign into an empty plot slot; or slot 1 reads atemporal and the entity is what the dwelling is made of.
- Loss when: slot 1 reads strongly temporal, or slot 5's obligatory count is already at or past the point where the required span is unreachable.
- Governs: slots 1, 2, 5, 8. Check the fraction — hierarchy lost against informativeness gained — before you place it.

### Remove an entity

Mechanism: removal frees charge and hands it somewhere. Where it goes is the decision; the removal is only its occasion.
- Gain when: the entity is exhausted on arrival (slot 5 says the user already has this information), or it is a claimant against accent #1 with nothing of its own.
- Loss when: what leaves is a **logos** — a content unit that answered a question the block ordering owes. Deleting blocks deletes the answers with them, and the cleanliness is paid for in the time needed to reconstruct state.
- Governs: slot 5.
- Single-occurrence rider, usable as a prompt and not as a rule: the demand to name an intercepting mechanism for every removed logos rests on one corpus occurrence (see `references/limits.md`). Raise it as a question, do not enforce it as a gate.

### Duplicate — full procedure, T7

The fork is closed, and it is closed by the observation that **duplication is an operation on accent, not on information.**
RU: дублирование — операция над акцентом, а не над информацией.

The cost exists only if the duplicate has to be *noticed* or *read*. Run it:

1. Ask where the eye already is when the duplicate would be read. A unit, a currency mark, a status read by an eye already standing in that cell costs approximately nothing — keep it and make the cell composite.
2. Ask whether the duplicate exists to make sure something is not missed. Insurance duplication is the designer's own uncertainty transferred into the layout and paid for out of hierarchy — strike it.
3. Compare against the alternative, which is almost always a legend or a key held elsewhere. The two costs are of different kinds and that is what decides: **a duplicate is damped by self-similarity once; a legend charges a loop on every pass.** Multiply the loop by slot 4's pass count and compare. Run the comparison; do not recall its usual outcome.
4. **The threshold.** Once more than one field has to be duplicated, the gain has ended — above it the duplicates themselves become a row that collapses into self-similarity, and the legend becomes the cheaper instrument.
- Counter-case: a carrier with a single pass has no loop to accumulate, so the legend's per-pass charge is charged once and the comparison inverts. Genuine cartographic density — where the value physically cannot be written into the object — is the other side.
- Governs: slots 4 and 8.

### Iconify, or replace an icon with text — full procedure, T8

Closed, and **not** by the type of sign. What decides is whether *this* audience already carries a cost-free sign-to-meaning link.
RU: решает не тип знака, а построена ли у этой аудитории связь «знак → смысл» без затрат.

1. First ask whether it is a duplicate at all. An icon that distinguishes neighbours in a row, or a label carrying information the icon does not, is not a duplicate — both stay and the question is closed.
2. If it is a duplicate: the icon survives only where the sign is a universal cliché for the audience named in slot 7, or where the product is imperative and used daily so the link is built once and then free.
3. Where the link is not built and will not be — games, free exit, a steady inflow of newcomers, domain pictograms — duplicating logic does not assist perception; it takes accent from the logic that does not duplicate. Remove the icon.
4. Where both are learned, remove whichever pulls accent off the key formulation.
5. **Hard rider:** removing text "so they will learn it later" is not permitted. Imperativeness amnesties costs paid once during learning; it never amnesties costs paid on every pass.
- Governs: slots 5 and 7, against slot 4's contact frequency.

### Take the mass solution, or depart from it — T9, and it is OPEN

Say out loud that this one is open. The criterion below is reconstructed from the author's account of banner blindness and is nowhere stated by him in a sentence; see `references/limits.md`.

The reconstruction that holds: **the element's job decides.**
RU: элемент, который должны найти, берёт массовое решение; элемент, который должен перехватить чужой взгляд, находит тиражный приём мёртвым.
- An element that must be **found** by someone already looking for it — navigation, a mechanic, a layout convention — takes the mass solution, and its familiarity is the asset. A departure here is paid for with a new logic you can articulate out loud.
- An element that must **intercept** a gaze looking for something else — a first screen, a banner, plot graphics — finds the mass device dead regardless of execution quality.
- Two modifiers: the width of the audience named in slot 7 sets the permitted range of departure — a mass audience narrows it, a narrow or expert one widens it; and repetition inside your own layout is not covered by this at all, that is self-similarity and belongs to the charge column.
- **What is not settled:** an element that must be both found and noticed. Mark that case a hypothesis and name the observation that would settle it.

### Divide / split

Mechanism: splitting manufactures a new step in the block ordering and therefore a new claimant.
- Gain when: the two things being split have different functions in the scenario and were merging into one spot.
- Loss when: you split points that were **already equal** and could have stayed collapsed into one unit of comparison. Check this before the split, every time; it is the cheapest error in the file to make and the most expensive to see afterwards.
- Governs: slot 8, and the block enumeration in Gate 2.

### Collapse into a row

Mechanism: a set of similar items becomes one unit of comparison and competes as a whole. It is the standard operation when slot 5's obligatory count is objectively high and the answer is to attack the count rather than the contrast.
- Gain when: the items are genuinely equivalent and none of them individually has to be found.
- Loss when: an item inside the row has to be found — devaluation sets in around the fourth or fifth element of a row (illustration of where the decline is noticeable, not a cap on row length), so a specific item late in a long row is written for nobody.
- Governs: slots 5 and 8.

### Push to another contour

Mechanism: contour decides membership, so moving an element changes which block ordering it sits on and who its neighbours are. It is the only way to reduce the obligatory block count without contesting the brief.
- Gain when: the information is genuinely lower in importance — the criterion is degree of importance, not frequency of need.
- Loss when: criticality was the real driver. Depth runs inverse to criticality: a datum whose omission decides the outcome belongs near the look-only contour however rarely it is consulted.
- Governs: slots 3 and 5.

### Hide behind a disclosure, a modal, or a click

Mechanism: the cost of a hidden control is multiplied by how often it is needed. A disclosure additionally destroys diagonal scanning of whatever it hides.
- Gain when: slot 6 says so. Hiding a control on a repeat visit is one of the four decisions advance and only advance settles.
- Loss when: the control is on the main path and needed per pass. A click that is not obligatory for the meaning is both non-obvious and extra — hover, automatic display, or a split into two blocks are the alternatives to weigh.
- Governs: slot 6 against slot 4's pass count.

### Confront / deconfront

Paired operations for raising one block against its neighbours.
RU: конфронтация — поднять блок; деконфронтация — увести соседей в нейтраль.
- **Confront**: raise the block, size first.
- **Deconfront**: take the neighbours down into the neutral instead. It buys the block exclusivity without adding noise, so it is the cheaper move whenever the screen is already loud — and it is unavailable if the paradigm was chosen without a neutral range, which is a cost incurred at Gate 1 and paid here.
- Gain and loss are decided by which side of the pair the accent budget can afford, not by preference. Reach the required span with the fewest operations.
- Governs: slot 8.

### Isolate

Mechanism: equal neighbours defocus into one spot at surface gaze and are both skipped. Space and functional zoning un-merge them; **colour does not** — applied to a merged pair it merges the colour too.
- Gain when: the pair is genuinely two things with two functions.
- Loss when: the pair should have been a row collapsed into one unit — then isolation manufactures the problem it appears to solve.
- Governs: slot 8.

### Separate equals (exclusion)

A different operation from accenting. It raises nobody; it makes two peers readable apart.
RU: эксклюзия — разведение двух равных по акценту блоков, а не усиление одного.
- Gain when: slot 5 says the block is a choice among equivalent options. There equal charges are *correct* and this is the whole of the work.
- Loss when: you are using it to avoid deciding which block is accent #1. Exclusion applied where an accent was owed leaves the ordering with no first step.
- The minimum grade available is difference by placement; that is the floor, not the answer. Full operation set in `references/charges.md`, Step 8.
- Governs: slots 5 and 8.

### De-energise (outline, monochrome, reduced detail)

Mechanism: lower an element's charge without removing it, when it is needed for one reason and harmful for another.
- Gain when: a second function nothing else carries would die with the element.
- Loss when: the element's whole value *is* its charge — an affect carrier on a dedicated screen. Then the fork is keep or remove, and de-energising produces exactly what a legal layer must never be: so weak that removing it would change nothing.
- Governs: slots 2 and 8.

### Remove from the carrier

Mechanism: the **only** operation that ends an accent loop. Dimming does not, because the renewal law restores the competitor after every hard cognitive act.
- Gain when: slot 4 reports renewal events and the competitor stays in the field of view during them.
- Loss when: the competitor is accent #1 and the permanence is the instrument you wanted.
- Counter-case with teeth: a carrier with **no** renewal events has no loops, so this operation has no loop to end and the diagnosis that motivated it is inapplicable. Say so rather than importing it. See `references/carriers.md`.
- Governs: slot 4.

### Concentrate or split the accent colour

Mechanism: accent comes from scarcity on the carrier, not from the intensity of the hue. Split across several points, the spots bind into one structure and the order of actualisation becomes unpredictable.
- Gain in concentrating when: the colour is carrying an **accent** — one target action.
- Gain in repeating when: the colour is carrying a **code** — status, category, ownership — read as a code by the audience in slot 7. Then repetition is the point and the accent must be built with another instrument.
- Before either, run the predicate check: is this colour already occupied by another meaning here, and does the hue drag a connotation the audience will read whether you meant it or not. A colour cannot hold two predicates at once.
- Governs: slot 7, then slot 8.
- Number: the roughly 90/10 split of neutral mass to accent is an illustration that floats across the corpus, not a norm. The inversion move — dropping the accent colour to the background and neutralising the target — rests on a single occurrence; hypothesis only.

### Stylise

Mechanism: stylisation converts a structural layer into the atemporal reading. A substrate that survived the four layer tests is *obliged* to be stylised rather than left as bare structure.
- Gain when: slot 2 names the affect carrier as "nobody", or the carrier is dedicated and met rarely, so the device is a one-time asset.
- Loss when: affect is already delegated, or the surface is permanently present and the toll is charged on every pass. Compute it: per-pass charge toll × slot 4's pass count, against the affect delivered where slot 2 named nobody.
- Loss unconditionally when it eats separability *and* a group-membership error is possible on this carrier — see the separability condition in `references/surface.md`.
- Governs: slots 1, 2, 4.

### Substitute one instrument of separation for another

Mechanism: when a budget is spent — nesting depth, layer count, accent — the move is to change the instrument, not to add another of the same.
- Gain when: a different instrument is genuinely free here: contrast, size, spacing, colour, a rule, a tab attached to the block edge.
- Loss when: the substitution duplicates a separation that already exists. Two instruments doing one job means one is a parasitic density by construction.
- Governs: Gate 3's four layer tests.

### Delegate sign outside the row of blocks

Mechanism: when you need more sign-load than the frame's hierarchy permits, stop competing for the charge budget and find a carrier that is not a block — framing, plot-bearing borders, the space between frames.
- Gain when: the sign column and the charge column contradict each other. This is the standard resolution of that contradiction.
- Loss when: it is being used to avoid deciding the accent. Never resolve the contradiction by lowering accent #1.
- Governs: slot 7 against slot 8.

## Back-translation is where this file ends

The charge list is not an output and neither is a signed operation. Every change of charge must land as a statable operation on form — outline icon becomes filled, icon larger, caption smaller, block off the carrier — and every operation must name the neighbour it charges.
RU: каждое изменение заряда назвать операцией над формой и назвать соседа, у которого заряд отобран.

Then the required output line, once per expensive decision:

```
<Ledger line> → <sign of the operation, in that line's terms> → <operation on form> → <neighbour charged>
```

Three of these accompany any proposal, per the output contract in SKILL.md.
