# Sequence and Accent

> Loaded when: Filling the charge column; or the complaint is "nothing stands out", "everything shouts", "it feels flat", "it's noisy"; or a block was added or removed.

This file computes one column of Gate 2: the charge of every block along the block ordering. It produces numbers, a gradient shape, and a set of operations on form. It does not produce a verdict on any operation — the sign comes from the Ledger. Run the steps in order; each one names what you write down.

## Step 1 — Name accent #1

Name the single thing the product exists for. Every other element is a claimant taking charge from it.
RU: акцент №1 — то, ради чего продукт существует; остальные элементы — претенденты, отнимающие у него внимание.

Accent goes to what would otherwise not be found, not to what is important in words. Size spent on what the user already knows is spent on nothing: reconstruct the path *to* this screen — the heading you arrived by is exhausted before the screen loads, and a peak on it is parasitic.
RU: путь до экрана исчерпывает его заголовок; акцент на исчерпанном — паразитный.

Write accent #1 down before any charge. If you cannot name it, the failure is in Gate 1, not here.

## Step 2 — Enumerate the blocks

Anything visually separated is a block and takes a step in the ordering.
RU: всё, что визуально отделяется, становится самостоятельным шагом секвенции.

- Outlines, rules and stray plates are blocks in their own right, not decoration. Adding one means agreeing to balance it against content by charge.
  RU: обводка и линия — самостоятельные блоки, а не украшение.
- A row of similar items collapses into one unit of comparison and competes as a whole; a small element next to five large ones is not competing with an element but with a block.
  RU: ряд однотипных схлопывается в одну единицу сравнения и конкурирует целиком.
- Before splitting anything, check you have not manufactured the problem by separating points that were already equal and could have stayed collapsed.

## Step 3 — Fix the block ordering

The block ordering is arbitrary to take and is simultaneously the actual chain of actualisation. Its parameter is hierarchy, which sets the transition probability between blocks. A scenario is orderings linked end to end.
RU: секвенция — произвольно взятая последовательность блоков и одновременно фактическая цепочка актуализации; параметр — иерархичность, задающая вероятность перехода; сценарий — скреплённые секвенции.

Contour decides membership before charge: second-contour elements sit on a different ordering and are not charged against first-contour neighbours. Criticality sets the contour — the higher the cost of missing a datum, the closer it sits to the look-only contour.
RU: критичность данных задаёт контур.

**Arbitrary-pair test.** Take arbitrary pairs of blocks, including pairs from overlapping sets, and for each predict which is actualised first. A model that only holds for one convenient route is not a model. Low hierarchy inside a pair means braking, and braking is where loops form.
RU: брать произвольные пары блоков, в том числе из пересекающихся множеств, и мерить между ними иерархичность.

## Step 4 — Assign charges

Give every block an absolute charge on a 1–10 scale (illustration — the unit is arbitrary; what matters is that charges are absolute, not shares of a hundred). A share-of-100 accounting hides exactly the thing you are measuring.
RU: абсолютный заряд, а не доля от ста.

Measure two things and only these two:
- **Span** between the extremes of the ordering. A narrow span means there is no hierarchy however tidy the work is. The job is to push the extremes apart, not to bring them together.
  RU: размах — расстояние между экстремумами заряда; узкий диапазон = иерархии нет.
- **Depth of the accent pits** — how far a block sits below its neighbours.
  RU: глубина акцентных ям.

Do not measure brightness. Brightness is one operation among several that changes a charge; it is not the quantity.

Collapse adjacent equal points into one point when describing the gradient. Do not collapse them when counting operations — two blocks still cost two edits.
RU: схлопывание точек правомерно при описании, но не при подсчёте правок.

Reach the required span with the fewest operations. Between compositions that look equally good, the one needing fewer edits wins.

**Adding a block is redistribution, not "+1 element".** Name whom the new block takes from, recompute the whole accent palette, and check the fraction: how much hierarchy was lost against how much informativeness was gained. Charge that arrives without this recomputation is how a palette quietly dies.
RU: добавление — переразложение палитры акцентов, а не прибавление элемента; проверять дробь иерархичность / информативность.

## Step 5 — Derive the required span (do not assume high is good)

Required hierarchy is a function of two things: the count of blocks the scenario makes obligatory, and the function of the block.
RU: требуемая иерархичность выводится из числа облигатных по сценарию блоков и из функции блока.

- Few obligatory blocks — around five to seven as a working threshold (illustration, not a norm) — a wide span is mandatory, and "it is a complex product" is not a defence.
- Objectively many obligatory blocks: do not turn up the contrast. Attack the *number* of blocks — contest a block with the client, push it to the second contour, or collapse a set into a row.
- The block is a choice among equivalent options (a menu, a toolbar, a comparison of peers): equal charges are **correct**. The work there is separating equals, not accenting one. This is a genuine second branch of the derivation, not a caveat on the first — take it to Step 5b, which runs to the same depth as the accent route.
  RU: при выборе среди равнозначных опций равные акценты правильны; задача — эксклюзия, а не акцент.
- Check the inverse error every time: the span may be wide and the peak on the wrong block. Diagnose declared accent against actual accent — what the author says is emphasised versus what the form emphasises.
- Also diagnose the *source* of flatness: flatness forced by the content is a different object from flatness the designer created. Only the second is a defect here; the first routes to Step 5b, not back to the block count.
- "Make hierarchy high, you never lose" is a starting assumption for a blank page, not an argument in a defence.

Write the required span into the Ledger as a computed value, and say which of the two inputs produced it — and if the answer is "flat is correct here", write that as the computed value too. A flat surface derived is a different object from a flat surface conceded.

## Step 5b — the flat branch: separating equals

Take this branch when the block's function in the scenario is a choice among equivalents, or when the obligatory count is objectively high and content-forced. Attacking the block count is the *other* branch and it does not apply here: there is nothing illegitimate to remove, and pushing a peak onto one peer misrepresents the scenario.
RU: плоскость, выведенная из функции блока, — не дефект; работа здесь — эксклюзия, а не акцент.

**5b.1 — Verify they are equals.** Two failure modes sit either side of this branch and take opposite operations.
- They are *not* equals — one is the value carrier and the flatness is the designer's. Go back to the accent route.
- They are equals but should not be separate at all — a row that ought to collapse into one unit of comparison. Separating manufactures the problem it appears to solve. Check this before any operation: did you split points that were already equal?

**5b.2 — Name what is currently gluing them.** Exclusion operates on the property that is merging the peers, and that property is what selects the operation. Read the merge, then choose:
- merged by *proximity* — they defocus into one spot → space and functional zoning; colour is the wrong instrument here and merges the colour too;
- merged by *self-similarity of form* — same silhouette, same weight, same rhythm → difference by placement is the floor grade of exclusivity available; above it, one exclusive property per peer, introduced as a system rather than per item;
- merged by *layer* — same substrate, same field, large among large → separate by the very property that would otherwise merge them, or move one peer's membership;
- merged by *contour* — they are only neighbours because both sit in the first contour → the separation is a contour reassignment, and it belongs to slot 3 rather than to this column.

**5b.3 — Check the peer count against the row law.** Exclusion does not scale indefinitely: past a point a set of separated equals is read as a row again, and devaluation inside a row sets in around the fourth or fifth element (illustration of where the decline is noticeable, not a cap). Above that, the honest operation is to give the set an internal structure — group the peers into a smaller number of units and separate *those* — rather than to keep separating individuals nobody reads.

**5b.4 — Exit condition, and it is not the span.** Each peer is findable by someone who is looking for that peer, no two of them defocus into one spot, and no peer has been raised above the others. If a peak has appeared, the branch failed: you accented instead of separating, and the scenario now claims a priority it does not have.
RU: выход из ветки — каждый равный находим, ни одна пара не слипается, ни один не поднят над прочими.

The operations named here are unsigned like every other; the Ledger signs them, and `references/operations.md` holds the counter-cases for each.

## Step 6 — Re-read the charges after each cognitive act

The frame's accent budget is closed **between** cognitive acts: inside one act you can only redistribute. A hard cognitive act resets it, and every block still on the carrier — except the one just handled — takes charge again.
RU: закон обновления: после сложного когнитивного действия все прежние блоки, кроме непосредственно предыдущего, заново набирают заряд.

Counts as a hard act: a click, a scroll, reading a paragraph, filling a field. Does not count: hovering, merely looking, a cliché icon. A heading the size of a paragraph is itself such an act and recomputes the palette of the whole screen.

List this artefact's hard acts, then walk the ordering again after each. Exhaustion drives the movement: a block wants to be exhausted and hand its charge on. An exhausted block that stays on the carrier pulls the gaze back to itself, and that pull is what accrues cost on every pass.
RU: принцип исчерпанности — блок стремится исчерпаться и передать заряд дальше.

**Motion never exhausts.** An element that ticks, animates or travels with the content refreshes its accent status every frame and wins the competition permanently, regardless of area; the user has to cut it out with the analytical gaze on every pass. Two animations additionally break each other's accent and do not sum.
RU: динамика — вечный победитель: акцентный статус обновляется каждый кадр, блок никогда не исчерпывается.

## Step 7 — Read the gradient

Ten shapes. Each gives a reading and a class of operation it opens. Each also carries a counter-diagnosis: the conditions under which exactly this shape is correct. A shape without its counter-diagnosis is a prohibition, and prohibitions are not this instrument.

1. **Narrow span across the whole ordering.** No hierarchy is being asserted; the order of traversal is undefined. Opens: spreading extremes, or attacking block count. Counter: flatness forced by the content, and the choice-among-equivalents block — there flat is right and the operation class is separation of equals, not accent.
2. **Adjacent equals merging into one spot.** Defocus the layout to colour spots and look again: two equal neighbours stick together and are both skipped. Opens: spatial isolation, or splitting into functional zones — not strengthening colour, which merges them further. Counter: a genuine row that should be collapsed into one unit of comparison rather than separated.
   RU: равные по акценту соседи слипаются в одно пятно при поверхностном просмотре и пропускаются оба.
3. **Accent pit.** An exhausted block still occupying its step, or a row where only the first and last are read at all. Opens: removal from the carrier, or shortening the row. Counter: a deliberately de-energised element that is needed for one reason and harmful for another.
   RU: акцентная яма.
4. **Loop.** Two points recharge each other across a cognitive act, and the cost accrues on every pass. Opens: taking the competitor off the carrier — dimming it does not help, because the renewal law restores it. Counter: a carrier with no renewal events has no loops and this diagnosis does not apply to it.
   RU: акцентная петля.
5. **Unexhaustible point.** Motion, or an element travelling with the content. Opens: fixing it, removing it, or stripping its accent weight. Counter: sometimes this is precisely the correct accent #1, and the unexhaustibility is the mechanism you wanted.
6. **Misplaced peak on the already-known.** The heading you arrived by, the familiar navigation bar, the user's own name on a screen reached by tapping their own icon. Opens: demotion, and reassignment of the freed charge. Counter: the element is not yet known to this audience at this entry point — then it is not already-known and the reading is wrong.
7. **Displaced accent.** An element pulling attention off the value carrier. Its cost is high early in a scenario and cheap at the end. Opens: demotion, relocation, contour change. Counter: late in a scenario, where the value is already delivered, the same displacement may be affordable.
   RU: смещённый акцент.
8. **Declared versus actual mismatch.** The emphasis the author claims and the emphasis the form produces disagree. This is a provable error, not a matter of taste — state both readings side by side. Opens: either the form changes or the declaration does. Counter: none as a reading; the counter-case is that the declaration was wrong, not the form.
9. **Sign-load / charge divergence.** A high-sign object sitting in a background slot, or a low-sign object in the plot slot. Read against the sign column — see `references/sign.md`. Opens: swapping slots, or repairing the sign rather than the charge. Counter: low sign-load placed deliberately on the background is a legitimate strategy, not a divergence.
10. **Exclusivity failure.** Large among large, a photo among photos, a pattern the same colour as the text over it. The element holds no accent regardless of its own weight, because self-similarity glues it to its neighbours into one structure. Opens: separate by the very property that would otherwise merge them. Counter: when the merge is wanted — a decorative field that should recede — the same self-similarity is the working device.
    RU: эксклюзивность — предпосылка акцента; самоподобие объединяет структуры и обесценивает вес.

**The analytical-gaze signal.** When your gaze catches on a place and you feel vague discomfort, do not rate it liked/disliked. Record that the analytical gaze fired and ask one question: is an accent needed at this point at all? The answer is usually no, and the complaint converts from taste into a hierarchy error with an address.
RU: включился аналитический взгляд — спросить, нужен ли акцент именно здесь.

## Step 8 — Choose the operations

Every operation below is unsigned. The Ledger signs it; `references/operations.md` holds the counter-cases.

- **Confront** — raise a block against its neighbours, size first.
  RU: конфронтация.
- **Deconfront** — take the neighbours down into the neutral instead. This buys the block exclusivity without adding noise, and it is the cheaper move whenever the screen is already loud.
  RU: деконфронтация — увести соседей в нейтраль.
- **Isolate** — cure merging equals with space and zoning, not with colour.
- **Separate equals (exclusion)** — a distinct operation from accenting; it does not raise anyone, it makes two peers readable apart. The minimum grade of exclusivity available is difference by placement. Which instrument above that floor is correct is derived at Step 5b from whatever is currently gluing the peers, not chosen.
  RU: эксклюзия — разведение двух равных по акценту блоков, а не усиление одного.
- **Remove from the carrier** — the only operation that ends a loop.
- **De-energise** (outline, monochrome) rather than delete, when an element is needed for one reason and harmful for another.
- **Concentrate the accent colour.** List every point where the accent colour occurs. Split across several points, the spots bind into one structure and the order of actualisation becomes unpredictable; concentrating it on the target action is the repair (the roughly 90/10 split of uniform mass to target action is an illustration that floats in the corpus, not a norm).
  RU: акцентный цвет дробить нельзя даже ради удобства.

## Step 9 — Judge at both scales

Read hierarchy twice: at screen scale and at block scale (the block as its own field of interaction). Expect the two verdicts to differ, and do not let either override by default — the block's function in the scenario resolves them.
RU: оценивать иерархию дважды — на уровне экрана и на уровне поля взаимодействия; вердикты могут расходиться.

A layout that reads on entry and falls apart inside one block is a block-scale defect, and the edit is addressed only to the broken scale.

## Step 10 — Back-translate into operations on form

The charge list is not an output. Every change of charge must be named as an operation on form — outline icon becomes filled, icon larger, caption smaller, block off the carrier — and every named operation must name the neighbour it charges. A charge changed without a named neighbour is arithmetic, not design.
RU: каждое изменение заряда назвать операцией над формой и назвать соседа, у которого заряд отобран.

Building a curve out of these numbers is optional and is never required of you or of the user; the list is what does the work. Do not add checks to this procedure from memory — several plausible-sounding ones rest on a single occurrence in the corpus and are quarantined in `references/limits.md`.

## Numbers used here, with status

- 1–10 charge scale — illustration; any absolute scale works, shares of a hundred do not.
- Two-to-four times between neighbouring charges — illustration, not a target.
- 90/10, 75/20/5, 70/30 accent distributions — illustrations; they float across the corpus.
- Five to seven scenario-obligatory blocks as the threshold for a mandatory wide span — illustration used as a working threshold.
- Devaluation setting in around the fourth or fifth element of a row — illustration of where the decline is noticeable, not a cap on row length.
- Two levels of **card** nesting — confirmed limit; the extension to other layer types is this skill's. It belongs to `references/surface.md`, not here.
- An accent step of 1.5–1.8 — **forbidden**; it is imported from the spacing progression and does not govern accent (see `references/limits.md`).
- Multiples of four and eight — **forbidden** here and everywhere (see `references/limits.md`).
