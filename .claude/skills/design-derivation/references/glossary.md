# Glossary

> Loaded when: A term is used or encountered; a reference file's RU: line needs unpacking; or a word from the corpus may be a homonym trap.

Use the EN handle in prose; use the RU term when you need the distinction the English loses, when you check a claim against the corpus, or when a stakeholder quotes the author. Never coin a synonym for a handle — if a handle reads badly, flag it.

Origin tag in the gloss: `idio.` = the author's own coinage or his re-specification of a common word, so the gloss below is the operative sense and a dictionary reading will mislead; `gen.` = ordinary design vocabulary, used as usual, listed because it carries a load here; `borr.` = imported from another field (semiotics, logic, philosophy) with a narrowed sense.

Every gloss states what you can do with the term. A term from which nothing is derivable is, by this model's own criterion, not a term.

| RU | EN handle | Gloss — what follows from it | ASR variants and traps |
|---|---|---|---|
| модус | modus | idio. Cell of a 3×3 matrix that types a **designer** and a **task** — where that person looks for solutions. Correction: modus and its defining properties are one level; the artefact-level decision is paradigm. Do not merge them, and do not use modus to read a work. | монус, музы, модус операнди |
| облигатные свойства | defining properties | idio. The properties by which a designer's modus is identified. Not a checklist to apply to a layout. | облигатный, облигаторные |
| парадигма | paradigm | idio. The artefact-level commitment — background, typeface, density, share of area under image, admissibility of stylisation. Levers to derive per brief, never a preset. | — |
| темпораль | temporal reading | idio. Score of how strongly this segment instrumentalises time, in either direction: acceleration *or* deliberate dwelling. Any claim made under it owes a measurement artefact. | темпораль, отпораль, темпоральность, тем параль |
| атемпораль | atemporal reading | idio. Score of how much affect this must carry itself. Read in parallel with the temporal reading, on the same work; the two legitimately diverge. | а темпораль, отемпораль, импораль, ампараль |
| аффект | affect | borr. Response produced by form and delivery; it wears out with contact frequency, so a daily surface cannot bank on it. | эффект (near-universal ASR substitution), афект |
| носитель аффекта | affect carrier | idio. Whoever already carries the impression — content, world, covers, the food itself. Name it and the interface stops topping up what is already delivered. | — |
| секвенция | block ordering | idio. An arbitrarily taken order of blocks that is simultaneously the actual chain of actualisation; hierarchy parameterises transition probability. Anything visually separated is a step in it. | сиквенция, секвенцы, сеенция |
| сценарий | scenario | gen. Block orderings linked end to end. Segment it into temporal and atemporal stretches before charging anything. | — |
| контур (1 / 2 / 3) | contour | idio. Level of access: read without action / ordering added / ordering replaced. Distributed by degree of importance of the information. Contour decides membership, so second-contour elements sit on a different ordering. | конту, контура |
| акцент / заряд акцента | accent / charge | gen.+idio. Charge is an absolute score, 1–10 (illustration), of a block's pull. Accent #1 is the thing the product exists for; every other block is a claimant taking from it. | акцентность, заря акцента |
| палитра акцентов | accent palette | idio. The map of charge across all blocks. The frame's budget is closed *between* cognitive acts and resets after one — it does not conserve across acts, and it is never a share of a hundred. | палитра акцента, разве совка |
| иерархичность | hierarchy | idio. Charge difference between neighbours and across the ordering. Derive the required amount from the count of scenario-obligatory blocks and from block function; equal charges are zero hierarchy, and sometimes correct. | ирархичность, иерор хищность |
| размах | span | idio. The distance between extreme charges plus the depth of the accent pits — the measured quantity, not brightness. Reach the required span with the fewest operations. | — |
| смещённый акцент | displaced accent | idio. An element pulling attention off the value carrier; expensive early in a scenario, cheap at its end. | смещен акцент |
| акцентная яма | accent pit | idio. An exhausted block still occupying the ordering, or a row where only the first and last are read. Diagnose it before touching contrast. | акцентная пустота |
| акцентная петля | accent loop | idio. Two points recharging each other across a cognitive act, paid on every pass. The competitor comes off the carrier; dimming it does not close the loop. | петечка, пробуксовка, петляние |
| принцип исчерпанности | exhaustion | idio. A block sheds its charge once used and hands it on — that hand-off is what moves the eye, and an unexhaustible element is a permanent winner. | принцип исчерпано, иср |
| закон обновления | renewal law | idio. After a hard cognitive act — click, scroll, reading a paragraph, filling a field, and not hover, not looking, and not a cliché icon — earlier blocks recharge. Re-read the charges after each. | принцип обновления |
| конфронтация / деконфронтация | confront / deconfront | idio. Paired operations: raise a block against its neighbours, or push the neighbours to neutral. Unsigned — a ledger line decides which is the gain here. | декон, деконт, деконтраст |
| эксклюзивность | exclusivity | idio. Unlikeness of a block to its neighbours, to the adjacent layer, and to ambient visual noise. Its absence means the element holds no accent regardless of its own weight. | эксклюзиве, инклюзив |
| эксклюзия | exclusion (separating equals) | idio. Separating two blocks of **equal** charge — a different operation from raising one, and the correct work on a choice among equivalents. | — |
| дублирование | duplication | idio. An operation on accent, not on information. It costs only when the duplicate has to be noticed or read. | — |
| аванс | advance | idio. The credit of attention the viewer extends to the carrier. Walk the ordering joint by joint: does this block add credit or burn it — it must add more than it spends. Calibrated by entry point; switched off under absolute temporality. | аваанс, обанс, ванс |
| знаковость | sign-load | idio. How many interpretants a **named** audience reads unambiguously, and what response they give. Orthogonal to charge and to detail; the audience is inside the definition. | знаково, слабознак, значковость |
| интерпретанта | interpretant | borr. (semiotics) A meaning this audience actually reads off a form. Counting them is how sign-load is measured, so an audience must be named before the count exists. | интерпретаторы, интерпретант |
| сюжетка / сюжетность | plot object / plot-load | idio. An image carrying a scene and an event — the fastest carrier of impression. Sign-load plus detail is plot-load. | сюжеточка, сюжетне |
| принцип достаточности | sufficiency principle | idio. Cut detail to the least at which the sign still reads, and take the invariant part off frame. Surplus detail is paid for with someone else's accent. | принципом достаточности |
| самоподобие | self-similarity | gen. Predictable repetition of a visual rule; it fuses structures into one layer and speeds exhaustion. Correction: symmetry is itself a self-similar structure — the load comes from breaking it, not from having it. | само подобие, сапо |
| визуальные правила | visual rules | idio. Countable units of form complexity — each new angle, weight, radius, colour, cut. Their number is the felt desynchronisation. | плюс одно правило |
| плотность / паразитарная плотность | density / parasitic density | idio. A countable unit of visual matter (line, substrate, outline, icon); parasitic when weighty and informationally empty. The evil is the parasitic kind, never density as such. | плотности, паразитарных плотностей |
| коэффициент информированности | informativeness ratio | idio. Information delivered against the number of densities delivering it; any duplication lowers it. First of the four ordered tests on an extra layer. | информированность |
| логос | logos | borr. The content unit that adds information and survives translation into meaningful text without describing itself. Also, in the drawing sense, the bare text block you lay out before designing. | лагос, лос, логосы |
| прогрессия отступов | spacing progression | idio. One ordered series — letter → space → line → paragraph → block — with a multiplier of about ×1.6–1.8 (norm; the author left the tolerance zone for exaggeration undefined), counted by eye, breakable on three named grounds only. | прогрессия уступов, теряем прогрессию |
| отделимость | separability | idio. Whether a block reads apart from its neighbour. The working criterion for any edit; glows and blurs eat it. | отдели мость, дискретность |
| нейтраль | neutral | idio. The muted range and its depth. Without it there is nothing to remove accent with except hacks; a deep neutral warms and needs cooling back. | увёл в нейтраль, нейтралии |
| предикат | predicate | borr. (logic) A meaning-bearing attribute tied to one entity. Introduce a colour or size code only when the predicate is unoccupied and carries no parasitic connotation. | свойство цвет |
| аккуратность | accuracy | idio. The account for edits that cannot change group membership, contour assignment, or the accent palette. Name it out loud, argue it atemporally, weigh it at about a fifth of the assessment (illustration) — not a half. | — |
| должное / сущее | what ought to be / what is | borr. (philosophy) Semantics names what ought to be; structure computes what is off the form with content ignored; the verdict is only the divergence, and content attaches last as a modifier of charge. | сущее, должное |
| схематоз | fake method | idio. Cutting every new case to a memorised template, and imitating method and measurement. Naming it is what stops "fewer densities, therefore faster". | смехотизация, схематоза |
| дефицитарность | deficiency | idio. An impoverished, under-thought solution presented as minimalism. Distinguishes emptiness left by default from low sign-load chosen as a strategy. | дефицитарное, нищая, нищие иконки |
| соберуха | reference-hoard | idio., pejorative. A store of references and accumulated experience in place of a derivable model. Correction: it names a genre of content, not a property of a layout — never use it to name the model-versus-practice axis. | соби руху, собе Руха |
| контрастные рисовки | contrastive variants | idio. Draw the competing variants and say why one wins; no answer means the choice was imprinting. The "spend ten minutes, don't be lazy" phrasing is another speaker's — see `references/limits.md`. | красная рисовка, трасу рисовки |
| герменевтика макета | layout hermeneutics | idio. Explanation fitted to a form that already exists. It sells a decision and generates none — a ledger written this way is discarded and re-derived, never edited. | гербиневтика |
| разумизм | context-free reasoning | idio. A question or a decision framed outside any concrete scenario; unanswerable by construction, so the move is to restore the scenario rather than answer. | разумист, сценарный разумизм |
| эрзац | ersatz | idio. Imitation of research, of method, or of a device. Apply it to your own output first; the channel name comes from it. | ирзац, эрзацум |
| дрейф | drift | idio. The pull of an industry or a team moving a designer into a foreign modus regardless of intent. A diagnosis about a designer, not about a work. | дрейв, дриф |
| имплицитная дизайн-система | implicit design system | idio. Unwritten knowledge of which orderings and priorities earn money; unreadable from the file and the UI kit. Where it may be hidden, do not liberalise the spec. | имплицитно система |
| поверхностный / аналитический взгляд | surface gaze / analytical gaze | idio. Parallel grasp of spots, lines and motion, against costly sequential inspection. Catching yourself reasoning about a block is the signal to ask whether an accent belongs there at all. | поверхностный Взгляд, срыв аналитического взгляда |

## Homonym traps

Each of these is a real corpus word with a second, unrelated sense. Check the sense before building anything on an occurrence.

- **топический** — the topical axis (scenario / balance / block) is one sense; "топический статус блока" inside the spacing progression is a different, unrelated sense about a block's rank in a composition.
- **блочная система** — in ordinary critique this names a kind of layout, not a mode of thinking, and is not the topical axis's block pole.
- **высокознак** — also the name of one of the author's own products; a share of the occurrences are advertising, not concept, and must not be counted as evidence for sign-load.
- **аппроксимация** — used for icon compression as well as for silhouette work.
- **инфляция** — monetary only; there is no "sign inflation" in the corpus.
- **мизансцена** — in the long drawing session this is ASR noise about joining blocks, not a term.
- **цех** — does not mean the "professional guild" addressee some summaries claim.

## Reading the transcripts

Automatic transcription corrupts the load-bearing vocabulary systematically: `аффект` almost always arrives as `эффект`, `атемпораль` as `отемпораль / импораль / ампараль`, `секвенция` as `сиквенция`, `иерархичность` as noise. Search by stem and by ASR variant together, and listen to the audio before treating any occurrence as evidence.

Three further cautions, all detailed in `references/limits.md`: several transcript pairs are one stream published twice, so matching passages are one piece of evidence and not two; a substantial share of the corpus is guest, co-host, judge and read-aloud speech that is not the author's; and terms marked there as single-occurrence — including the nested-sequence term, the primordial-versus-actual accent pair, and the escapism remark — are hypotheses, not method.

**Retracted vocabulary.** `трек` appears in older material as one pole of a distinction the author later cancelled publicly, folding everything into `секвенция`. Do not rebuild that distinction from an old occurrence; see `references/limits.md`. `манна внимания` is not his term at all — it entered through reconstruction and carries no sense here.
